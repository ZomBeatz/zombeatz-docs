# Export and share

Share details about your hardware: paste a description into a chat, hand someone a spreadsheet, or print a spec sheet or sell sheet. Logins and secrets are never exported.

![Export & Share](/img/harm/export.png)

1. **Devices:** filter by location, holder, type and status, then tick the ones you want (**Select shown** ticks them all).
2. **Include:** choose what goes in: basics, serial, location and rack position, CPU/RAM/PSU/OS, storage and drives, other installed parts, network (IPs, MACs, VLANs), services, slots and bays, cabling, login names (never passwords), purchase and warranty, and notes.
3. **Get it:**
   - **Copy for AI / chat (Markdown):** tidy text to paste into a chat with a person or an AI assistant when you're asking for help.
   - **Markdown**, **Spreadsheet (.csv)** or **JSON** files.
   - **Spec sheet (PDF):** a printed overview of each device.
   - **Sell sheet (PDF):** hides internal details (location, IPs, hostnames) and adds a title, subtitle, condition, asking prices and a contact line.
   - **QR labels:** stickers for the selected devices.

PDFs use your browser's print dialog: choose **Save as PDF**.

A single device's **⋯ > Export / PDF / sell sheet** opens this page with just that device picked. Each list in HaRM (devices, parts, networks) can also be downloaded as CSV from its **⋯** menu.
