# A small office closet

You look after a small business's network closet and computers. You end up with every device, its asset tag and warranty in HaRM, QR labels that open each one, and notes the next person can follow.

## On a server or the office PC (once)

1. Run HaRM on something that's always on: [An always-on home server](./home-server) works the same in an office. A PC that's on all day works too: [Installing and updating](../installing).
2. Make the places: the building, the closet, the rack, and each desk or room: [Places and racks](../places-and-racks).
3. **Discover** brings in what's on the network: [Discover](../discover).
4. For each device, fill in **Asset tag (your own label)**, **Purchased** and **Warranty until** on its **Edit** form.
5. Save the router, switch, firewall and printer logins: [Logins](../logins). Turn on 2FA in **Settings > Locking and security**.

## Labels (once)

1. **Export & Share > QR labels**: tick the devices and choose your label size (62 × 29 mm, 2 × 1 in, 1 × 1 in, or a full sheet).
2. Stick one on each device. Scanning it with a phone opens that device in HaRM.

See [Scanning and labels](../scanning-and-labels#qr-labels).

## Every day

- **Home** shows warranties running out and devices marked broken: [Home and search](../home-and-search).
- Note every change on the device you touched ("Replaced toner", "New port 12 for reception"): [Notes and tasks](../notes-and-tasks).
- The **Audit log** shows who changed what, and when.

## Good to know

- Everyone who uses HaRM shares the master password, so give it only to people who should see the logins.
- For a handover, **Export & Share > Copy for AI / chat** gives a readable summary of the whole closet.
- Keep the backups somewhere off the premises: [Data and backups](../data-and-backups).
