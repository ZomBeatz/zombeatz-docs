# A pile of spare parts

You have boxes of drives, memory, cards and cables, and want to know what you've got, where it is, and what it's worth. You end up with every part in its box, with values, and with HaRM showing which spares fit which device.

## Set up the places (once)

1. Make a location for where the boxes live, like **Garage**, and a holder for each box or shelf (**Drive bin**, **Wire shelf**): [Places and racks](../places-and-racks).
2. Optional: set up a service in **Settings > AI Assist** so photos go straight to it. Without one, AI Assist uses copy and paste with any AI app: [AI Assist](../ai-assist).

## Box by box

1. Open the holder's page and press **AI Assist here**.
2. Photograph the box with the labels showing. Several photos are fine.
3. Get the AI's answer (sent for you, or by copy and paste), and press **Read reply**.
4. Check the list: fix anything it misread, untick what you don't want, and press **Add**. Everything goes into that holder, with its price new and what it sells for now.
5. Serials the AI couldn't read? Open the part and use the camera button next to **Serial**: [Scanning and labels](../scanning-and-labels).

A big stack of the same thing (twenty drives)? **+ New > New part from label** with **Save & add another** is quicker than photos.

## Every day

- **Spare Parts** lists what's in stock, installed or out for RMA, with totals like "14.4 TB of drives in 5 drives": [Parts and storage](../parts-and-storage).
- A device's **Hardware** tab shows its free bays and slots, and which spares fit them.
- Installing a part? **Install** moves it into the device and out of the bin.

## Good to know

- Prices from AI Assist are estimates from the web. They're marked with where they came from and when, so you can check them.
- Parts and devices photographed together are sorted for you: drives and memory become parts, a switch becomes a device.
- Logins and other secrets are never sent to an AI service.
