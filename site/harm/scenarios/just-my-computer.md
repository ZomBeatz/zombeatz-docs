# Just my computer

HaRM on one desktop or laptop, used in the browser on that computer. You end up with your hardware in HaRM and nothing shared on the network.

## On the computer (once)

1. Install HaRM: [Installing and updating](../installing).
2. Open **Zom's HaRM**. In the control panel, untick **Let phones and other devices connect**: only this computer can reach HaRM. HTTPS can stay on or off.
3. Press **Start**, then **Open HaRM**.
4. Create your master password and keep the recovery code safe: [Installing and updating](../installing#the-first-start).
5. Follow the setup guide: a place, a holder, your network, a first device.
6. Fill in this computer's hardware: open its device, **⋯ > Import specs… > This computer** ([Import specs](../import-specs)).
7. Tick **Start HaRM when I log in** so it's always ready ([The desktop app](../desktop-app#settings)).

## Every day

- Open HaRM from the tray icon, or from **Open HaRM** in the control panel.
- Search with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>, add with <kbd>N</kbd>: [Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard).
- Note what you change on a device as you go: [Notes and tasks](../notes-and-tasks).

## Good to know

- HaRM only runs while the computer is on. For something that's always there, see [An always-on home server](./home-server).
- Want your phone later? Tick **Let phones and other devices connect** and **Use HTTPS**, press **Restart**, then follow [My computer and my phone](./computer-and-phone).
- Your data is in your own data folder, and HaRM backs it up daily: [Data and backups](../data-and-backups).
