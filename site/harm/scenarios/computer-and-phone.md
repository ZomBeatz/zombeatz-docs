# My computer and my phone

HaRM runs on your computer, and your phone uses it over your home Wi‑Fi while the computer is on. You end up with HaRM as an app on your phone's home screen, with the camera ready for labels.

## On the computer (once)

1. Install and start HaRM: [Installing and updating](../installing). Leave **Let phones and other devices connect** and **Use HTTPS** ticked.
2. Give the computer a fixed address in your router (a DHCP reservation), so the phone always finds it.
3. Tick **Start HaRM when I log in** in the control panel ([The desktop app](../desktop-app#settings)).
4. Press **Phone setup**. Keep it open: its checklist follows your phone.

## On the phone (once)

1. Join the same Wi‑Fi as the computer.
2. Scan the first QR code in **Phone setup** with the phone's camera and trust HaRM's certificate. The steps for Android, Samsung Galaxy, iPhone and iPad are next to the code: [Phones and tablets](../phones-and-tablets), [iPhone and iPad](../iphone-ipad).
3. Scan the second QR code. HaRM opens with no warning.
4. Unlock with your master password, then add HaRM to the home screen.

The checklist on the computer ticks each step off, and says which step to fix if one fails.

## Every day

- At the rack, search for a device by name, IP or serial and check its ports: [When to grab your phone](../phones-and-tablets#when-to-grab-your-phone).
- Adding parts? **+ New > New part from label** opens the camera: [Scanning and labels](../scanning-and-labels).
- Tick off tasks and add notes where you're working: [Notes and tasks](../notes-and-tasks).

## Good to know

- The phone reaches HaRM only while the computer is on and HaRM is running.
- Away from home, connect to your home network with a VPN first. Don't forward HaRM's port to the internet.
- If the computer's address changes, the control panel offers **Fix certificate**; phones keep trusting HaRM.
