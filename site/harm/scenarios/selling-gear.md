# Selling old gear

You have servers, switches or parts you don't use anymore and want to sell them. You end up with prices for each, a sell sheet to post or print, and HaRM knowing what's gone.

## Find out what it's worth

1. Mark what you'll sell: open each device and set its status to **Spare**, or keep parts **In Stock**.
2. Use [AI Assist](../ai-assist) with web search on (photos of the gear) to fill in **Original price (new)** and **Current value**. Or type what you've seen it sell for into **Current value** on the **Edit** form.
3. Check the **Purchased** and **Warranty until** dates; buyers ask.

## Make the sell sheet

1. **Export & Share** (in the menu): tick the devices you're selling.
2. Choose **Sell sheet (PDF)**. It lists each one with its specs, condition and asking price, ready to print or save as PDF.
3. Want text for a listing site instead? **Copy for AI / chat** gives a short description of each.

See [Export and share](../export-and-share).

## After it sells

- Set the device's status to **Sold**, and add a note with who bought it and for how much ("Sold to Jordan, $120").
- Its logins can be deleted, or kept and marked old.
- Sold devices stay in HaRM, out of the way, so you remember what you had.

## Good to know

- Values from AI Assist are estimates; check a few recent sold listings before you set a price.
- Wipe drives before they leave: add a **Task** on each one ("Wipe drives") so it shows on **Home** until it's done.
