# Accessibility

How HaRM works with a keyboard, screen readers, bigger text and other settings on your device. If something gets in your way, please tell us: [Contact and feedback](./contact).

## Keyboard

- Everything can be reached with <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Tab</kbd>, with a visible ring around the button or field that has focus.
- Dialogs close with <kbd>Esc</kbd>, and the first field in them has focus when they open.
- Shortcuts for search, filtering, adding and help: [Keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard).

## Screen readers

- Buttons that only show an icon (close, show password, copy) have a spoken name.
- Dialogs are announced as dialogs, and messages like "Saved" or "Copied" are read out when they appear.
- Statuses are always written as words ("Broken", "Spare", "In stock"), never shown by color alone.

## Color and contrast

- **Light**, **Dark**, or **Match my computer**: **Settings > Appearance**.
- Text meets a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 in both looks.
- Colors that mean something always come with words: rack drawings have a key naming each color (servers, network, storage, power, other gear, accessories), and warnings have an icon and a sentence.
- Printed pages and labels are always light, whatever the look on screen.

## Text size and zoom

- HaRM follows your browser's text size, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>−</kbd> zoom it.
- On phones, it follows the phone's text size, and buttons are at least 40 pixels tall so they're easy to tap.

## Motion

With **reduce motion** turned on in your device's settings, HaRM turns off its small animations (toasts sliding in, the logo's movement).
