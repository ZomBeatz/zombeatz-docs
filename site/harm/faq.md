# Questions

Quick answers to what people ask most. Each links to the page with the full story.

## Is Zom's HaRM free?

Yes, free and open source under the MIT license. There's no paid version and no account. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zoms-HaRM).

## Do I need an account or the internet?

No account. You need the internet only to download HaRM and, if you want them, the parts library and an online AI service. Everything else works on your home network with no internet at all.

## Where is my data stored? Does ZomBeatz Media see it?

On your own computer or server, in HaRM's data folder ([where exactly](./data-and-backups#where-your-data-is)). Nothing is sent to ZomBeatz Media or anyone else.

## Can I try it without entering my own gear?

Yes: [the live demo](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zoms-HaRM/) runs HaRM in your browser with a made-up home lab. See [Try the demo first](./installing#try-the-demo-first).

## Does it work on my phone, iPhone or iPad?

Yes, as a home-screen app over your Wi‑Fi, once the phone trusts HaRM's certificate. See [Phones and tablets](./phones-and-tablets) and [iPhone and iPad](./iphone-ipad).

## Can I use it away from home?

Connect to your home network with a VPN, and HaRM works as usual. Don't forward its port to the internet. See [Setting up a server](./server).

## Can several people use it?

Everyone who knows the master password can use it from their own browser or phone at the same time. There are no separate user accounts yet.

## Does HaRM use AI?

Only if you ask. **AI Assist** turns photos into inventory, either by copy and paste with any AI app, or by sending the photos you pick to the service you set up. Logins are never sent. See [AI Assist](./ai-assist).

## What if I forget the master password?

Use the recovery code on the unlock screen (**Forgot your password? Use the recovery code**). Without either, saved logins can't be recovered; the rest of your inventory isn't affected. See [Security and privacy](./security).

## How do I get my data out?

**Export and share** copies devices as text, spreadsheets (CSV), JSON, spec sheets and sell sheets, and **Settings > Data & backups** downloads the whole database. See [Export and share](./export-and-share).

## Can I bring in a spreadsheet I already have?

Not directly yet. [Discover](./discover) and [Import specs](./import-specs) fill in most things without typing, and [AI Assist](./ai-assist) can read a photo of a printed list. See [Moving in from a spreadsheet](./scenarios/from-a-spreadsheet).

## Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is it safe?

That appears for apps that aren't signed with a paid certificate yet. Click **More info > Run anyway**. The installer is built by GitHub from HaRM's public code. See [Installing and updating](./installing#windows).

## What's new, and how do I update?

[What's new](./whats-new) lists every version. To update, open the newer installer; your data is kept. See [Installing and updating](./installing#updating).

## How do I report a bug or suggest something?

**Settings > About > Send feedback**, or email [info@zombeatzmedia.com](mailto:info@zombeatzmedia.com). See [Contact and feedback](./contact).
