# Zom's HaRM

**Zom's HaRM** (Hardware and Rack Manager) keeps track of your computers and network gear: where each one lives, what's inside it, how it's cabled and networked, how to log into it, and what still needs doing. Think of NetBox or a rack spreadsheet, made for a home lab or a small office.

It runs on your own computer or server, and your phone uses it over your Wi‑Fi. Nothing is sent to the internet unless you choose an online AI service.

![HaRM's Home page with what needs attention and the open tasks](/img/harm/home.png)

<a class="zb-cta" href="https://zombeatz.github.io/Zoms-HaRM/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Try the live demo</a>

The live demo is the real HaRM with a made-up home lab, running in your browser. Nothing to install, and your changes stay in that browser.

## Start here

- **New?** [Quick start](./quick-start) takes you from download to your first rack, device and phone in about 15 minutes.
- **Just looking?** [The live demo](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zoms-HaRM/) has two Proxmox hosts, a NAS, VMs, spare parts and tasks to click through.
- **Your situation, step by step:** [Set-up scenarios](./scenarios/) for one computer, a home server, a Proxmox lab, a pile of spares and more.
- **Want everything?** The basics pages cover the everyday things, the advanced pages cover every option, and [Settings](./settings) lists every setting.

## What you can do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Know where everything is** | Locations, racks with front and rear views, shelves, bins and piles ([Places and racks](./places-and-racks)) |
| **Keep every detail** | Serials, status, specs, purchase, warranty and value for each device ([Devices](./devices)) |
| **Track spare parts** | Drives, memory, cards and cables, drive bays and what fits where ([Parts and storage](./parts-and-storage)) |
| **Map the network** | Subnets and VLANs, an IP map, ports, services and cables ([Networks and cables](./networks)) |
| **Virtual machines** | VMs and containers on their hosts, clusters, and how full each host is ([Virtual machines and clusters](./virtual-and-clusters)) |
| **Log in faster** | Encrypted passwords and 2FA codes, with one tap to the iDRAC, iLO or web page ([Logins](./logins)) |
| **Fill it in without typing** | Specs over SSH, your router's device list, Proxmox, label scanning and photos ([Import specs](./import-specs), [Discover](./discover), [Scanning and labels](./scanning-and-labels), [AI Assist](./ai-assist)) |
| **Remember what you did** | Notes and tasks on every device, rack and room ([Notes and tasks](./notes-and-tasks)) |
| **Share and sell** | Text for a person or an AI, CSV, spec sheets, sell sheets and QR labels ([Export and share](./export-and-share)) |
| **Plan a build** | PC parts lists with compatibility checks ([PC Builder](./pc-builder)) |
| **Use it anywhere at home** | A phone app, a desktop control panel, or a server for the whole house ([Phones and tablets](./phones-and-tablets), [The desktop app](./desktop-app), [Setting up a server](./server)) |

## Where it runs

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11** | A normal installer from [the latest release](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zoms-HaRM/releases/latest) ([Installing and updating](./installing)) |
| **Linux** | `.deb`, `.rpm` or AppImage from the same page |
| **Home server or NAS** | Docker or a Linux service ([Setting up a server](./server)) |
| **Phones and tablets** | Android, iPhone and iPad, as a home-screen app over your Wi‑Fi ([Phones and tablets](./phones-and-tablets)) |
