# Home and search

**Home** is where HaRM opens: what needs a look, your open tasks, and a few numbers. **Search** finds anything from any page.

![Home](/img/harm/home.png)

## Home

- **The numbers:** devices (and how many are hooked up), open tasks and issues, spare parts, raw storage, CPU cores and memory.
- **Needs attention**, most urgent first: devices that stopped answering (see [Status](./status)), IP addresses used by more than one device, devices marked **Broken** or **In Repair**, warranties ending within 90 days, active devices marked not hooked up, and logins not changed in over a year. When there's nothing, it says so.
- **Tasks and issues** from every device, rack and room. Tick one to mark it done.
- **Rack space**, **Spare parts on hand** and **Storage**, once you have any.
- **More** at the bottom: devices by status and type, and recent activity.

## Search

Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac) or click the search box at the top, and type at least two letters: a name, IP, MAC, serial, part number, login name, note text, network or cluster.

![Search results as you type](/img/harm/search.png)

On pages with a list (devices, parts, logins), <kbd>/</kbd> filters just that list instead. Every key: [Keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard).

::: tip On a phone
Search sits at the top of every page, so it's the quickest way to find a device while you're standing in front of it.

<div class="phone-shots">

![Search on a phone](/img/harm/phone-search.png)

</div>
:::

- Results appear as you type. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click one.
- Picking a result opens that exact thing: an IP opens its interface on the device's **Network** tab, a login opens its **Open** window, a part opens its form.
- Enter with nothing picked shows every result. A search with exactly one result opens it straight away.

## + New

**+ New** (top right) adds anything: a device, spare part, login, network, rack or shelf, or location. It can also start from a photo: **New part from label**, **New device from label**, **AI Assist: photos to inventory**, or scan a HaRM QR label to open its device.

![The + New menu](/img/harm/new-menu.png)

On a phone, the scan items at the top open the camera straight away.
