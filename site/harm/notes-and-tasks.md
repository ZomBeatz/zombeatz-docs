# Notes and tasks

Every device, rack, room, network, build and cluster has a journal: notes, tasks, work you've done, and issues. Open tasks and issues from everywhere show on Home and on the Tasks page.

## Adding to the journal

Type into the box at the top of the **Overview** tab (or on a rack, room or cluster page) and press Enter. Pick the kind first:

- **Note:** something to remember ("Rear door sticks").
- **Task:** something to do ("Update iDRAC firmware").
- **Did:** work you've done ("Replaced PSU 2"), so you have a history.
- **Issue:** something wrong ("Fan is noisy").

Tick a task or issue to mark it done. The **⋯** on an entry pins it to the top, edits it, or deletes it. Marking a drive as failed adds an issue by itself.
