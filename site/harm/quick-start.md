# Quick start

This gets you from download to your first rack and device, with your phone connected, in about 15 minutes.

1. **Install HaRM.** On Windows, extract the zip to your user folder and double-click **Install HaRM.cmd**. On Linux, run `./install.sh`. Details: [Installing HaRM](./install).
2. **Start it.** Open **Zom's HaRM** from the Start Menu or app menu, press **Start**, then **Open HaRM**. See [First start](./first-start).
3. **Create your master password** and write down the recovery code. The password encrypts your stored logins.
4. **Follow the setup guide** that opens: add a location (your house, a room), a holder (a rack, a shelf, a bin), your networks, and a first device. You can skip any step.
5. **Fill in a device's hardware** without typing: open it, press **⋯ > Import specs…** and pick **Over SSH** or **This computer**. See [Import specs](./import-specs).
6. **Bring in the rest of your network:** **Discover** reads your router's client list, a UniFi console, Proxmox, or scans your network. See [Discover](./discover).
7. **Connect your phone:** in the desktop app press **Phone setup** and scan the two QR codes. See [Phones and tablets](./phones-and-tablets).

## What next

- Add **notes and tasks** as you work on things: type into the box on any device and press Enter. See [Notes and tasks](./notes-and-tasks).
- Put your **spare parts** in: [Parts and storage](./parts-and-storage), or photograph a pile with [AI Assist](./ai-assist).
- Save your iDRAC, iLO and SSH **logins**, so **Open** takes you straight in: [Logins](./logins).
