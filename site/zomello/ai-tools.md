# AI tools (optional)

::: info There's no AI inside Zomello
Zomello doesn't contain, connect to or send anything to any AI service, and nothing here is needed to use it. These tools only **write text you can copy** into an assistant you already use (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a local model…), and **read its reply back** if you paste it in. If you'd rather not use AI at all, simply ignore this page: nothing in Zomello depends on it.
:::

## What they're good for

- Turning a goal ("plan a kitchen remodel") into a board full of cards.
- Breaking a big card into steps.
- Sorting a messy bucket by priority, or estimating how long things take.
- Getting a status summary of a project.
- Handing a list of bugs or tasks to a coding assistant as a numbered work order.

## Open the AI tools

- On a board: **⋯ > AI tools (prompts & export)…**
- On a bucket: **⋯ > Ask AI for cards here…** or **Export bucket for AI…**
- On a card: right-click > **Hand to AI…**
- With several cards selected (**Ctrl click**): **Hand to AI…**

![The AI tools window](/img/zomello/ai-tools.png)

The window has three tabs.

### 1. Ask AI for cards

Pick **Add cards to a bucket** or **Build a new board**, choose where, and describe what you need ("cards for setting up a home media server"). Zomello writes a prompt that explains your board (its buckets, labels, people, fields and the **About this board** text) and the reply format. **Copy** it (or **Save as file**) and paste it into your assistant.

### 2. Export for AI

Choose what to send (the whole board, one bucket, or the cards matching your current filter) and what you'd like done:

- **Do them** (a numbered work order, for example for a coding assistant)
- **Work through them** (for example bugs)
- **Break them down**
- **Prioritize & estimate**
- **Summarize status**
- **Something else…** (your own request)
- **Just the export** (only the cards as text)

### 3. Apply AI reply

Paste the assistant's whole answer. Zomello finds the part it can use and shows what it will do: add cards (with sub-tasks linked), build a board, or update cards (progress, priority, dates, labels, notes, checklist items, bucket). **Nothing changes until you press Apply**, and **Ctrl Z** undoes it.

A plain bulleted list works too: each line becomes a card, and indented lines become its checklist.

## Your privacy

Zomello never sends anything anywhere. What you copy into an assistant follows that assistant's own privacy rules, so leave out anything you wouldn't share there.
