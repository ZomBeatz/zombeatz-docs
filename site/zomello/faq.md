# Questions

## Is Zomello free?

Yes: the apps and Zomello Server are free, with no ads.

## Do I need an account or the internet?

No. Zomello works offline with no account. Your boards are saved on your device. You only need a network to connect your phone and computer, or to use a Zomello Server.

## Does Zomello use AI?

No. There's no AI built in, and nothing is sent to any AI service. The optional [AI tools](./ai-tools) only prepare text you can copy into an assistant yourself.

## Where are my boards stored? Does ZomBeatz Media see them?

On your own devices (and on your own Zomello Server, if you run one). ZomBeatz Media never receives anything. See [Data and backups](./data-and-backups) and the [privacy policy](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zomello/privacy.html).

## Can my phone and computer use the same boards?

Yes, over your home Wi‑Fi: [Your computer's boards on your phone](./phone-and-computer). From anywhere: [Away from home](./scenarios/away-from-home).

## Can several people work on one board?

Yes, with a [Zomello Server](./server): accounts, shared boards and live updates. To just give someone a copy, send a [board file](./board-files).

## Can I keep some boards private?

Yes: a board's menu > **Private: only on this device**. See [Sharing between your devices](./sharing-between-devices).

## How do I get my boards out of Zomello?

**Save as board file** for one board, **Settings > Data > Export JSON** for everything, or **Copy work order** for readable text.

## I deleted something by mistake.

**Ctrl Z**, or **Undo** on the message right after. Archived things are under **Archive**. On a computer, **Settings > Data & backups > Automatic backups** has a copy from every 30 minutes.
