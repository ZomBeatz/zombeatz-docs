# Views

Every board can be shown five ways. Switch with the buttons next to the board's name; each board remembers its own view. New boards use the view chosen in **Settings > Appearance > New boards open in**.

## Board

![Board view](/img/zomello/board.png)

Buckets side by side, cards you drag between them.

**Group by** (top right) shows the same cards in columns by **Progress**, **Priority**, **Assigned to**, **Label** or **Due date** instead of by bucket. Dragging a card between those columns changes that property: drag it to "Urgent" and the card becomes urgent. (This works for progress, priority, person and due date; grouped by label, cards stay where they are.)

## List

![List view](/img/zomello/list.png)

Every card in a table with its bucket, progress, priority, dates, people, labels and checklist count.

- Click a column title to sort; click again to reverse.
- Change bucket, progress or priority right in the row.
- **Add a task to "To do"…** at the top adds a card quickly.

## Calendar

![Calendar view](/img/zomello/calendar.png)

A month of cards by due date.

- Drag a card to another day to change its due date.
- The **+** on a day adds a card due that day.
- **Unscheduled** lists cards without a date: drag one onto a day to give it a date, or drag a card back to clear it.
- Events from your own calendars (Google, Outlook, Apple) can appear here too: see [Calendar feeds](./calendar-feeds).
- On a phone the month fills the screen and unscheduled cards are underneath.

## Timeline

![Timeline view](/img/zomello/timeline.png)

Cards with dates as bars from start to due date, grouped by bucket. Pick **2 weeks**, **6 weeks** or **3 months**, move with the arrows, or **Today** to jump back. Click a bar to open the card. Cards without dates aren't shown (the count says how many).

## Charts

![Charts view](/img/zomello/charts.png)

- **Totals:** all tasks, not started, in progress, completed (with a percentage) and late.
- **Overall progress** as one bar.
- **Time tracked** against estimates, and the tasks with the most time.
- **By bucket**, **By priority** and **By assignee** bars.

## Filters (all views)

![Filter menu](/img/zomello/filter.png)

- **Filter cards…** narrows the board as you type (titles, descriptions, checklists, notes and file names). On a computer, **/** jumps there.
- **Filter** narrows by due date (late, today, next 7 days, no date), progress, priority, label and person. A dot on the button shows a filter is on.
- **Show related tasks** (in a card's menu) shows only that card and the cards linked to it.
