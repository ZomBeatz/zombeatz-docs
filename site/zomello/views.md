# Views

Every board can be shown five ways. Switch with the buttons next to the board's name; each board remembers its own view.

## Board

Buckets side by side, cards you drag between them. **Group by** (top right) shows the same cards grouped by progress, priority, person, label or due date instead of bucket.

On a phone, one bucket fills the screen; swipe sideways for the next.

## List

Every card in a table: sort by any column, add a task at the top, see buckets, dates, people and progress at a glance.

## Calendar

![Calendar view](/img/zomello/calendar.png)

A month of cards by due date. Drag a card to another day to change its date. **Unscheduled** lists cards without a date; drag one onto a day to give it one, or drag a card back there to clear its date. On a phone the month fills the screen and unscheduled cards are underneath.

## Timeline

Cards with start and due dates as bars, grouped by bucket. Choose 2 weeks, 6 weeks or 3 months. Cards without dates aren't shown (the count is at the top).

## Charts

Totals by progress and bucket, overall progress, and what's late.

## Filters

**Filter cards…** searches the board as you type. The **Filter** button narrows by label, priority, progress, person or due date (late, today, this week). On a computer, **/** jumps to the filter box.
