# The desktop app (Windows and Linux)

Extra things the computer version of Zomello can do.

## Several windows

- Right-click a board > **Open in new window** (or middle-click it in the menu) to give it its own window. Handy for keeping one board on a second screen.
- Windows stay in sync: a change in one shows in the others right away.
- Zomello Servers you've added open in their own window too, and so do a connected phone's boards.

## Keep it running

- **Settings > Desktop app > Keep running in the tray when I close the window.** Closing the window then leaves Zomello in the system tray (near the clock): reminders keep coming and your phones stay connected. Open or quit it from the tray icon.
- **Start Zomello when I log in** starts it quietly in the tray.
- Only one copy runs at a time: opening Zomello again brings the existing window forward.

## Files

- **Drag files** from your file manager onto an open card to attach them. They're copied into Zomello's own storage.
- **Drag a board file** (`.zomello`) onto the window to add it. Trello and Planner exports can be dropped into **Settings > Data > Import a board**.
- **Double-click** a `.zomello` file to open it in Zomello.
- **Save as board file…** saves to your Downloads folder; click **Show** to see it.

## Zoom and text

- **Ctrl +**, **Ctrl −** and **Ctrl 0**, or hold Ctrl and scroll, anywhere in the app.
- Spell check underlines mistakes in every text field (right-click for suggestions). On Linux, install a dictionary such as `hunspell-en_us` if it isn't there.

## Notifications

Reminders and the daily summary appear as your system's notifications. If none show, check **Settings > Reminders**, use **Send a test notification**, and make sure your system allows notifications from Zomello.

## Phones and tablets

The desktop app can share its boards with your phones and tablets on the same Wi‑Fi, and show their boards in its sidebar. See [Your computer's boards on your phone](./phone-and-computer).

## Where things are stored

`%APPDATA%\com.zombeatz.zomello` on Windows, `~/.local/share/com.zombeatz.zomello` on Linux. See [Data and backups](./data-and-backups).
