# Zomello

Zomello is a planner for any occasion: boards with columns ("buckets") of cards you drag along as work moves, like Trello, with the task details of Microsoft Planner. It runs on Windows, Linux, Android, iPhone and iPad. Your boards stay on your own devices, or on a Zomello Server you run.

## What Zomello can do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Boards and cards** | Buckets you drag cards between, sections to group boards, checklists, labels, priorities, dates, people, notes, files and links on every card |
| **Views** | Board, List, Calendar, Timeline and Charts, plus Home and My Tasks across all boards |
| **Phone and computer** | The phone app has its own boards and can open your computer's boards over your Wi‑Fi, encrypted |
| **Moving and sharing** | Drag boards between devices, copy and paste cards, share boards between your devices, keep some boards private |
| **Board files** | Save a board as a `.zomello` file and send it to anyone |
| **Zomello Server** | Accounts, shared boards and live updates for a team or family, on your own server |
| **No account, no tracking** | Nothing goes to ZomBeatz Media. Automatic backups keep your work safe |
