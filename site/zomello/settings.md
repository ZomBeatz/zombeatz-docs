# Settings

Everything in **Settings** (bottom of the menu), section by section. The buttons at the top jump to each section.

![Settings](/img/zomello/settings.png)

## Appearance

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Theme** | System (follows your device), Light, Dark |
| **Accent** | The color of buttons and highlights: Zomello blue or any other color |
| **Density** | Compact, Normal, Comfortable |
| **Week starts on** | Sunday or Monday (calendars and "this week") |
| **New boards open in** | Board, List, Calendar or Timeline view |

Each board also has its own look: [Customizing a board](./customize-board).

## Features

Choose how much Zomello shows, so it can start simple and grow with you. Pick a starting point, then turn single groups on or off:

| Preset | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Simple** | Boards, buckets and cards, checklists, labels, people, due dates, reminders, the calendar, My Day and search |
| **Standard** | Simple, plus Planning extras, Linking tasks, More views and Board looks |
| **Everything** | Every group below |

| Group | What it adds |
|---|---|
| **Planning extras** | Start dates and repeating tasks (also "every monday" typed in a title) |
| **Linking tasks** | Sub-tasks, tasks that block each other, related tasks |
| **More views** | List, Timeline and Charts (Board and Calendar are always there) |
| **Board looks** | Backgrounds, card covers and per-bucket card display |
| **Time tracking** | The timer, focus countdowns, estimates and tracked time |
| **Custom fields** | Your own fields on cards |
| **Bucket automation** | [Bucket rules](./buckets#bucket-rules) and work-in-progress limits |
| **AI tools** | Work orders and [AI tools](./ai-tools) |

**Turning a group off never deletes anything.** It hides the buttons and menu items that start it. A card that already uses a hidden feature (a start date, tracked time, a custom field) still shows it, and turning the group back on brings everything back.

New installs ask in the welcome guide: Standard on computers, Simple on phones and tablets. If you've used Zomello before this setting existed, everything stays on.

## Accessibility

See [Accessibility](./accessibility): zoom, high contrast, reduce motion, strong focus outline, larger click targets, readable spacing, underline links.

## People

The people you can assign cards to. **Me** is you: it decides what's on Home and in My Tasks, and which reminders are yours. Click a name to rename it everywhere; the × removes it; **Add a person…** adds one.

## Reminders

- **Notifications** on or off.
- **Daily summary** of late and due-today tasks, at a time you choose.
- **Send a test notification** to check they show up.

## Adding tasks

- **Read repeats in titles:** end a new task's title with how often ("every monday", "monthly on the 1st") and Zomello sets its Repeat. On unless you turn it off. Dates and times in titles are always read. See [typing dates and repeats in the title](./home-and-search#quick-add). New in version 0.5.4.

## Templates

Your card templates: rename or delete them. See [Templates](./templates).

## Servers

- **Zomello Servers:** add one by address, or **Find servers on this network**. Each opens in its own window on a computer, or under its own heading in the phone app's menu. See [Zomello Server](./server).
- **Boards on your computer** (phone and tablet apps): connect to your computer, scan its QR code, and **Let my other devices see this phone's boards**. See [Your computer's boards on your phone](./phone-and-computer).
- **Back up this phone's boards on my computer** (phone and tablet apps), optionally with private boards locked by a password. See [Device backups](./device-backups).

## Desktop app (computer only)

- **Keep running in the tray when I close the window:** phones stay connected and reminders keep coming.
- **Start Zomello when I log in:** it starts quietly in the tray. (Installed from the Microsoft Store: turn it on in Windows Settings > Apps > Startup.)

## Phones & tablets (computer only)

Let your phones and tablets use this computer's boards: turn it on, allow it through the firewall, install the certificate on each device, pair devices, and manage paired devices. Step by step: [Your computer's boards on your phone](./phone-and-computer). **Device backups** lists the copies your phones and tablets asked this computer to keep: see [Device backups](./device-backups).
