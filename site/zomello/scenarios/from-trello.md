# Switching from Trello or Planner

## What things are called

| Trello | Planner | Zomello |
|---|---|---|
| Board | Plan | Board |
| List | Bucket | Bucket |
| Card | Task | Card |
| Checklist | Checklist | Checklist |
| Labels | Labels | Labels |
| Members | Assigned to | Assigned to (People) |
| Power-ups: Calendar, Custom Fields | Schedule view, Charts | Built in: Calendar, Timeline, Charts, Custom fields |
| Workspace | Group | Section |

## Bring your boards over

1. Export from Trello (**⋯ Menu > Print, export, and share > Export as JSON**) or Planner (**⋯ > Export plan to Excel**).
2. In Zomello: **Settings > Data > Import a board**, choose the file, check the preview, **Import as a new board**.
3. Repeat for each board, then drag them into sections.

Step by step: [Coming from Trello or Planner](../import).

## Things you'll like

- Repeating tasks, reminders and time tracking without add-ons.
- **My Tasks** and **Home** across all boards.
- Works offline, with no account; your data stays with you.
- [Move boards](../moving-boards) between your phone and computer, or share them on [your own server](./family-server).
