# My computer and my phone

Keep your boards on your computer and use them on your phone too, over your home Wi‑Fi, encrypted. The phone app also keeps its own boards.

**You need:** Zomello on your computer (Windows or Linux) and the phone app, both on the same Wi‑Fi.

## On the computer (once)

1. **Settings > Phones & tablets** > tick **Allow access from phones and tablets on this network**.
2. **Let it through the firewall:** click **Allow through firewall** and confirm (Windows asks; Linux asks for your password).
3. **Settings > Desktop app > Keep running in the tray**, so the phone can connect even after you close the window.

![Settings > Phones & tablets on the computer](/img/zomello/phones-tablets-settings.png)

## On the phone (once)

1. **Install the computer's certificate.** It's what makes the connection secure. On the computer, under **Security certificate**, click **Put it on a device**, and scan that QR code with the phone's camera app. The page that opens has the download and the steps for Android or iPhone/iPad.
2. **Pair:** on the computer, click **Pair a device**. A new QR code appears (it works once and lasts 10 minutes).
3. In the Zomello app: **Settings > Servers > Scan the QR code on your computer**, and scan it.
4. Allow Zomello to find devices on your network if the phone asks.

![The certificate QR code on the computer](/img/zomello/certificate-qr.png)

## Every day

- The phone's menu has **This phone** and **Computer**. Tap a board under **Computer** to open it.
- The app reopens wherever you were. Away from home, it opens the phone's own boards and offers **Try again** later.
- While the phone is connected, its own boards show in the computer's sidebar under its name.

## Good to know

- **Moving boards** between them: drag a board onto the other place's heading ([Moving boards](../moving-boards)).
- **Keep some phone boards off the computer:** mark them **Private: only on this device**.
- **The computer's address changing** breaks the connection now and then: ask your router for a fixed address for it (a "DHCP reservation"), or pick the right address in **Settings > Phones & tablets**.
