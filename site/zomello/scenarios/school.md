# School or studying

Keep track of classes, homework and exams.

1. **Make a section** "School" and a **board per class** (or one board with a bucket per class).
2. Buckets: **To do**, **Working on it**, **Handed in**.
3. **Add assignments** with their **due date**; set **priority** for big ones.
4. **Exams:** a card with a **checklist** of chapters to review, and a **reminder** the evening before.
5. **The Calendar view** shows every due date in the month; drag a card to move its date.
6. **Home** each morning: late, due today, next 7 days, across every class.
7. **Focus timer:** start a 25-minute countdown on the card you're working on; it also tracks your study time.
8. **Repeating** weekly readings: **Repeat > Weekly**.

**Tip:** add your school's calendar feed (if it has an iCal link) to a board in **Customize > Calendars** to see lectures and holidays next to your assignments.
