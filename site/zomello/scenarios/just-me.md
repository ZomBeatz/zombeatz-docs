# Just me, on one computer

The simplest setup: Zomello on your Windows or Linux computer, nothing else.

1. **Install** Zomello from the [latest release](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zomello/releases/latest) ([Quick start](../quick-start) has the details).
2. **Make sections** for the parts of your life: **+ Section** > Work, Home, Personal.
3. **Make a board** in each (the **+** next to a section's name).
4. **Set yourself as Me** in **Settings > People** (the welcome guide does this), so Home and My Tasks show your tasks.
5. **Turn on reminders:** **Settings > Reminders**, then **Settings > Desktop app > Keep running in the tray** and **Start Zomello when I log in**, so reminders arrive even when the window is closed.
6. **Start each day on Home:** late, due today, next 7 days.

**Your data:** it's all on this computer, with a backup every 30 minutes ([Data and backups](../data-and-backups)). Copy the data folder now and then to a USB stick or cloud drive for extra safety.

**Later:** add your phone with [My computer and my phone](./computer-and-phone).
