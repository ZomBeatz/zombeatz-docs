# All my devices together

An Android phone, an iPad and a computer that can all see and edit each other's boards while they're at home, with some boards kept private to one device.

1. **Connect each phone and tablet to the computer** first: [My computer and my phone](./computer-and-phone).
2. **On each phone or tablet that should share:** **Settings > Servers** > tick **Let my other devices see this phone's boards** (or "this iPad's").
3. **On the iPad**, open your computer's boards: the menu now shows your Android phone (if it's connected and sharing) with its boards. Tap one to open and edit it there. **Back to your computer's boards** returns.
4. **Keep something private:** on the device that has it, open the board's menu > **Private: only on this device**. It never leaves that device: not to the computer, not to the other devices. A lock marks it.

## How it works

Your computer passes boards between your devices; nothing is copied or stored. When a device leaves the Wi‑Fi or closes Zomello, its boards disappear from the others until it's back.

## Several people, one computer?

If a partner or roommate pairs their phone with the same computer, their shared boards would show to you too. Use **Private** for anything personal, or give everyone their own accounts with [a Zomello Server](./family-server) instead.
