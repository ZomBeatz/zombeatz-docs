# Household chores

A chores board that refills itself, assigns jobs, and reminds people. Works on one device, or shared on a [family server](./family-server).

1. **Make a board** "Chores" with buckets **This week**, **Doing**, **Done**. In **Done**'s menu, set **Cards moved here become > Completed**.
2. **Add people:** **Settings > People** > add each person (on a server, everyone with an account is already there).
3. **Add the chores as cards:** "Take out the trash", "Vacuum", "Laundry"…
4. **Make them repeat:** open each card > **Repeat**: Weekly (pick the day), Every 2 weeks, Monthly. When someone completes it, the next one appears with the next date.
5. **Assign them:** open a card > **Assign**.
6. **Reminders:** a **Reminder** on the cards that need one ("Bins out" Tuesday 7 pm), plus **Settings > Reminders > Daily summary** in the morning.
7. **Everyone checks My Tasks** to see just their chores.

## Nice touches

- **Customize > Show on cards > Assigned to: Avatars with names**, so the board shows who does what at a glance.
- A **checklist** on big jobs ("Clean kitchen": counters, floor, fridge).
- **Group by > Assigned to** in Board view shows a column per person.
- **Card aging** (in Customize) fades chores nobody has touched in a while.
