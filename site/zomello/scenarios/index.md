# Set-up scenarios

Pick the one closest to you. Each is a short, start-to-finish recipe.

## Where your boards live

| Scenario | Good for |
|---|---|
| [Just me, on one computer](./just-me) | Simplest setup |
| [Just a phone](./phone-only) | No computer needed |
| [My computer and my phone](./computer-and-phone) | See and edit your computer's boards on your phone at home |
| [All my devices together](./all-my-devices) | Phone, tablet and computer seeing each other's boards, with private boards kept private |
| [Away from home](./away-from-home) | Reaching your computer's boards from anywhere |
| [A family or roommates](./family-server) | Everyone with their own account, shared chores and plans |
| [A small team](./small-team) | Shared projects on your own server, reachable from anywhere |

## Moving in

| Scenario | Good for |
|---|---|
| [Switching from Trello or Planner](./from-trello) | Bringing your boards over |
| [Sending a board to a friend](./send-a-board) | Sharing without any server |

## Ways to use Zomello

| Scenario | Shows off |
|---|---|
| [Household chores](./household-chores) | Repeating tasks, people, reminders, My Tasks |
| [A project with a deadline](./project) | Buckets as stages, dates, Timeline, linked tasks, custom fields |
| [School or studying](./school) | Sections per class, due dates, the Calendar, the focus timer |
