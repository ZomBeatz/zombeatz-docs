# Just a phone

Zomello works fully on its own on an Android phone, iPhone or iPad. No computer, account or internet needed.

1. **Install** from Google Play or the App Store.
2. In the welcome guide, enter your name and pick a look.
3. Open the menu (three lines, top left) > **+ Board**. Group boards with **+ Section**.
4. **Press and hold** cards and boards for their options; **swipe sideways** between buckets.
5. **Settings > Reminders:** allow notifications so card reminders and the daily summary can reach you.

**Keep a copy:** everything lives inside the app and is deleted if you uninstall it. Now and then, use a board's **⋯ > Save as board file…** to keep a copy in Google Drive, iCloud or your Downloads.

**Later:** connect it to your computer with [My computer and my phone](./computer-and-phone), or move boards there with [Moving boards between devices](../moving-boards).
