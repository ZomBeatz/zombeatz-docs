# A project with a deadline

Plan something with stages and dates: a website, a move, an event.

1. **Make a board** with a bucket per stage: **Ideas**, **In progress**, **Review**, **Done**. Set **Cards moved here become** on In progress and Done.
2. **Write About this board** (**Customize**): the goal, the deadline, who's involved.
3. **Add cards** with a **start date** and a **due date**; assign people and priorities.
4. **Break big cards down** with a checklist, and **spin off** the big steps into their own cards (sub-tasks).
5. **Link dependencies:** on a card that can't start yet, **Link a task > Blocked by**. It shows **Blocked** until the other is done.
6. **Add custom fields** if you need them: Owner, Budget, Size.
7. **Watch the plan:** the **Timeline** shows what overlaps; **Charts** show progress and what's late; **Filter > Due: Next 7 days** shows what's coming.
8. **Track time** on cards with the timer, and set an **estimate**; Charts compare them.

![The Timeline](/img/zomello/timeline.png)

When it's done: **⋯ > New board with this layout** reuses the setup for the next project, and **Archive** puts this one away.
