# Using Zomello on a phone or tablet

The Android, iPhone and iPad apps have everything the computer has, arranged for touch.

![Home on a phone](/img/zomello/phone-home.png){.phone}

## Getting around

- **The menu:** tap the three lines at the top left. It slides in with Home, My Tasks, your boards and Settings. Tap outside it to close.
- **Places:** the menu has a heading for each place your boards live: **This phone** (or **This iPad**), **Computer** and any **Zomello Servers**. Tap a heading to fold it. Tap a board under another place to go there.
- **Search** (magnifier) and **Quick add** (**+**) are at the top right.
- **Back:** the phone's back gesture or button closes windows and menus first, then goes back a page.

## Touch gestures

| Gesture | Does |
|---|---|
| **Tap** a card | Opens it |
| **Press and hold** a card, bucket, board or section | Opens its menu (the same as right-click on a computer) |
| **Press and hold, then drag** | Moves a card, bucket or board |
| **Swipe sideways** on a board | Next or previous bucket |
| **Drag a board onto another place's heading** | Moves or copies it there ([Moving boards](./moving-boards)) |

## Phone-friendly details

- One bucket fills the screen on a board; the Calendar fills the width with unscheduled cards below.
- Windows such as a card or Paste cards open full screen.
- Keyboard hints are left out on touch screens.
- Your phone keeps its own theme and accessibility settings, even when it shows your computer's boards.

## Saving and sharing files

- **Save as board file…** opens the system's Save screen (Android) or share sheet (iPhone and iPad). Afterwards **Share** sends it by email or chat.
- **Paste cards…** fills in from the clipboard when the phone allows it; otherwise press and hold in the box and choose **Paste**.

## Without installing the app

If Phones & tablets is on in the desktop app, any phone's browser can open your computer's boards: scan the QR code from **Pair a device**. On Android, Chrome then offers **Install app**; on iPhone and iPad, use **Share > Add to Home Screen**. It works like an app, but only while the phone can reach your computer.
