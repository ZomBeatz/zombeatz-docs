# Moving boards between devices

Move a board (or copy it) from your computer to your phone, or back. It brings its buckets, cards and attached files.

## On the phone

1. Open the menu. Each place has a heading: **This phone**, **Computer**, servers.
2. Press and hold a board, then drag it onto another place's heading and let go.
3. Choose **Move** (it leaves where it was) or **Copy** (it stays there too).

![Dragging a board from Personal and choosing Move or Copy](/img/zomello/move-board.webp){.phone}

Zomello briefly switches to the other place, adds the board there, and opens it.

You can also press and hold a board (without dragging) and choose **Move to…** or **Copy to…**.

## On the computer

When your phone is connected (same Wi‑Fi, Zomello open on it), it shows in the computer's sidebar with its boards.

![A connected phone in the computer's sidebar](/img/zomello/computer-sidebar-phone.png)

- **Computer to phone:** drag one of your boards onto the phone's name, then choose **Move** or **Copy**. Or right-click the board > **Move to (phone)** / **Copy to (phone)**.
- **Phone to computer:** drag one of the phone's boards into your own boards. Or right-click it > **Move to this computer** / **Copy to this computer**.

::: info Attached files
Files attached to a phone's board live on the phone. Bringing that board over from the computer copies everything except those files. To move them too, do it from the phone (it sends them along).
:::

## Moving cards

Single cards (or a selection) can go to a board on another device too. In the card's menu, choose **Move to device** or **Copy to device**, then the device and the board.

- The cards go into the bucket with the same name on that board, or its first bucket. Labels come along by name (missing ones are added), and custom fields that match by name and type keep their values.
- From a phone, you can send cards to your computer or a Zomello Server; from your computer, to a phone that's connected right now.
- On a phone, Zomello then opens the other place and shows the board with the cards in it.

New in version 0.5.4.

## Good to know

- A move only removes the board from the old place once it has been safely handed over.
- If the other place can't be reached, nothing changes and you get a message.
- Copying a board that already exists there makes "Board name (2)".
