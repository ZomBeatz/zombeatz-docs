# Home, My Day, My Tasks and search

## Home

![Home](/img/zomello/home.png)

Home gathers what needs attention across every board:

- **Late:** past their due date
- **Due today**
- **Next 7 days**
- **In progress:** assigned to you and started
- **Boards:** every board by section, with how much is done and how much is late

Click any task to open it, or a board to go there. On a computer, **H** goes Home from anywhere.

## My Day

![My Day](/img/zomello/my-day.png)

What to do today, from every board, in one list:

- **Late:** past their due date.
- **Today:** due today, or running across today (a start date before and a due date after). Cards with a time come first, in time order.
- **Added by you:** anything you add with **Add to My Day** (in a card's menu) or the box at the top. Added cards leave My Day at midnight, so each morning starts fresh.
- **Done today:** what you finished today.
- **Suggestions:** cards due or starting tomorrow, in progress, or important with no date. **+ My Day** adds one.

It shows cards assigned to you (set who you are in **Settings > People**) or to no one. On a computer, **D** opens My Day. New in version 0.5.4.

## My Tasks

![My Tasks](/img/zomello/my-tasks.png)

Everything assigned to you, across all boards.

- Group by **Due date**, **Board**, **Progress** or **Priority**.
- **Include unassigned** adds cards nobody has. **Show completed** adds finished ones.
- **Add a task for (you)…** at the top: type, pick a board, **Enter**.
- On a computer, **M** opens My Tasks.

"You" is whoever is set as **Me** in **Settings > People**.

## Search

![Search](/img/zomello/search.png)

**Ctrl K** (or the magnifier on a phone, or **Search** in the menu) searches board names, card titles, descriptions, checklists and notes as you type. Tick **Archived** to include archived cards. Use the arrow keys and **Enter**, or click a result.

## Quick add

![Quick add](/img/zomello/quick-add.png)

Add a task to any board without leaving what you're doing: **N** on a computer, **Quick add** at the top of the menu, or **+** at the top on a phone.

Pick the **board** and **bucket**, optionally a **due date** and **priority**, and whether to **assign it to you**. **Add task** saves it; **Add & open** opens it right away. Your templates are offered here too.

**Type the date in the title.** End it with a day or time and Zomello sets it for you: "Dentist tomorrow 3pm", "Call Sam friday at 2:30", "Report due Oct 28", "Pay rent on the 1st", "Taxes in 3 days". It shows what it found under the title; the × keeps those words in the title instead. Only the end of the title is read, so "Plan Friday party" stays as it is. This works wherever you add a task: Quick add, **Add card** in a bucket, List, My Day and My Tasks. New in version 0.5.4.

## Focus timer

**Focus timer** in the menu starts a countdown: 5, 10, 15, 25, 30, 45 or 60 minutes, or your own length (like `20`, `1h` or `1:15`), with an optional note of what you're focusing on. Start one from a card (**Time > Focus**, or right-click > **Focus countdown**) and it also tracks the time on that card. You get a chime and a notification at the end. The countdown shows in the menu while it runs.

## Help

**Help** at the bottom of the menu (or **?** on a computer) opens:

- **Show the welcome guide** again, for a one-minute tour.
- **Open the full guide** (this site).
- Answers to common questions, and every keyboard shortcut.

## Fold the menu

On a computer, the **«** button at the top of the menu (or **[**) folds it into a slim strip of icons; click again to bring it back.

## Undo

**Ctrl Z** undoes the last change (**Ctrl Shift Z** or **Ctrl Y** redoes it). Deleting or archiving something also shows a message with an **Undo** button.
