# iPhone and iPad

Zomello on iPhone and iPad works like the Android app, with a few things done the Apple way. It's coming to the App Store soon.

## The basics

- The menu calls the device **This iPad** (or **This phone** on an iPhone), with a heading for each other place: your computer and any Zomello Servers.
- **Press and hold** anything for its menu; **press, hold and drag** to move cards, buckets and boards. Swipe sideways between buckets.
- On an iPad, the menu stays open beside the board, like on a computer.
- Each iPhone and iPad keeps its own look and accessibility settings.

## Permissions Zomello asks for

| Permission | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Local Network** | The first time you connect to your computer or look for servers | To reach the Zomello app on your computer, or a Zomello Server, on your Wi‑Fi |
| **Camera** | The first time you tap **Scan the QR code on your computer** | Only to read the pairing code; nothing is saved |
| **Notifications** | When you turn on reminders | To show the reminders you set |

Changed your mind? **iPhone Settings > Zomello** (Camera, Local Network, Notifications), or **Settings > Privacy & Security > Local Network**.

## Connecting to your computer

The same as on Android ([Your computer's boards on your phone](./phone-and-computer)). Installing the computer's certificate on an iPhone or iPad takes two extra taps:

1. Open the certificate link from the computer's **Put it on a device** QR code in **Safari** and allow the download.
2. **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management**: tap the downloaded profile, then **Install**.
3. **Settings > General > About > Certificate Trust Settings**: turn on full trust for the Zomello certificate.

The setup page on the computer shows these same steps.

## Board files

**Save as board file…** opens the share sheet: **Save to Files** keeps it on the device or in iCloud Drive, or send it by AirDrop, Mail or Messages. To open one, **Settings > Data > Import a board** and pick it from Files.

## Without the app

On your computer's boards in Safari, **Share > Add to Home Screen** puts Zomello on the home screen like an app. See [Using a browser instead of the app](./phone-and-computer#using-a-browser-instead-of-the-app).
