# Boards and sections

A **board** is one project, area or list: "Website Launch", "Home Projects", "Weekly Chores". You can have as many as you like.

## Make a board

- **+ Board** at the bottom of the menu, type a name, **Enter**.
- Or the **+** next to a section's name, to make it inside that section.
- Or **⋯ > New board with this layout** on an existing board to reuse its buckets, labels and settings without the cards.

New boards start with **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**, and open in the view chosen in **Settings > Appearance > New boards open in**.

## The board menu

Right-click a board in the menu (press and hold on a phone), or use **⋯** at the top right of the board:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Open** / **Open in new window** | Opens it (a separate window on a computer; middle-click does the same) |
| **Rename** | Also: double-click the name in the menu, or click the title on the board |
| **Category…** | Puts it in a section, or makes a new one |
| **Star** | Pins it under **Starred** at the top of the menu |
| **Customize…** | Icon, color, background, what cards show and more ([Customizing a board](./customize-board)) |
| **AI tools…** / **Copy work order** | Optional helpers for using an AI assistant ([AI tools](./ai-tools)) |
| **Paste cards…** | Adds cards you copied ([Copying and pasting cards](./copy-paste-cards)) |
| **Duplicate** | A copy with all its cards |
| **New board with this layout** | A copy without cards |
| **Move to… / Copy to…** | Another device or server (phone and tablet apps) |
| **Private: only on this device** | Never shown on your other devices ([Sharing between your devices](./sharing-between-devices)) |
| **Save as board file…** | A `.zomello` file to send anyone ([Board files](./board-files)) |
| **Archive** / **Delete** | Archive hides it (find it under **Archive**); Delete removes it, with **Undo** right after |

## Sections

Sections group boards in the menu and on Home, like notebooks: Work, Home, School, Personal.

- **Make one:** **+ Section** at the bottom of the menu.
- **Put a board in it:** drag the board onto the section, or right-click > **Category…**.
- **Fold it:** click the arrow next to its name.
- **Rename or remove:** right-click the section's name > **Rename category** or **Remove category (keeps the boards)**. Its boards then move to **Other boards**.
- A section stays in the menu when its last board leaves, so you can fill it again later.

## Reorder boards

Drag boards up and down in the menu. Drop one between two boards of another section to move it there in the same step.

## Archive

**Archive** (in the menu) lists archived boards, buckets and cards. Click **Restore** to bring one back, or delete it for good from there.
