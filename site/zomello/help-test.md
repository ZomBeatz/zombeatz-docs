# Help test Zomello

Zomello is a free planner for boards, lists and cards, with a calendar, reminders and a My Day page. It keeps your boards on your own devices: no account, no ads, no tracking.

Before it goes live in Google Play and the App Store, we're looking for a small group of people to try it on their own phones and tablets and tell us what works and what doesn't.

<a class="zb-cta" href="https://forms.gle/q9XuPyY55MmLki9BA">Sign up to test</a>

## What it involves

- **About two weeks.** Google asks that testers keep the app installed for 14 days in a row. A few minutes a day is plenty: plan your week, a trip, chores or a project.
- **Tell us what you notice.** Anything confusing, broken, or missing. A sentence is enough; there's no survey to fill in.
- **Free, always.** Zomello costs nothing, and testing doesn't sign you up for anything else.

## Who we're looking for

| Device | What you need |
|---|---|
| **Android phone or tablet** | A Google account (the one you use for Google Play) |
| **iPhone or iPad** | The free TestFlight app from Apple, and your Apple ID email |
| **Windows, Mac or Linux computer** (optional) | Nothing extra. Download it from the [releases page](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zomello/releases) |

Any experience level is welcome. People who have never used a planner app are just as helpful as people who use one every day.

## What happens after you sign up

1. You get an email within a day or two with your invitation: a Google Play link for Android, or a TestFlight invite for iPhone and iPad.
2. Install Zomello from that link and use it the way you normally would.
3. Send feedback any time: reply to the email, or use [Contact and feedback](./contact).
4. When testing ends, your app keeps working and updates normally from the store.

## Your information

The sign-up asks for your name, email and device, only so we can send your invitation and reach you about the test. We don't share it or use it for anything else, and you can ask us to delete it at any time. Zomello itself collects nothing: see the [privacy policy](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zomello/privacy.html).
