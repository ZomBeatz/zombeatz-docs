# Contact and feedback

Found a problem, or have an idea? We'd like to hear it.

## Report a problem

The more of these you include, the faster it can be fixed:

1. **What you did**, step by step ("opened a card, added a due date, pressed Save").
2. **What happened**, and what you expected instead.
3. **Your device and system:** Windows 11, Ubuntu 24.04, an Android phone (which one), iPhone or iPad, or Zomello Server with Docker.
4. **Your Zomello version:** **Settings > About**.
5. **A screenshot** if something looks wrong. Please use example data, not anything private.

Send it one of these ways:

- **In Zomello:** **Help > Send feedback** (or **Settings > About**). It starts an email with your Zomello version and device already filled in.
- **GitHub:** [open an issue](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zomello/issues/new). You can see what others reported and follow the fix.
- **Email:** [info@zombeatzmedia.com](mailto:info@zombeatzmedia.com)

## Suggest a feature

Same places. Say what you're trying to do, not just the feature: "I want to see two boards at once" helps more than "add split view", because there may be a better way to solve it.

## Before you write

- [Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting) covers the most common problems.
- [Questions](./faq) answers the usual "can it…?".

## Privacy

Zomello never sends your data to ZomBeatz Media, so we can't see your boards to look into a problem. Only what you choose to include in a report reaches us. [Privacy policy](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zomello/privacy.html).
