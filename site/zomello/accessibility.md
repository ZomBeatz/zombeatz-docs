# Accessibility

Zomello can be adjusted to how you see, read and move. Everything here is in **Settings > Accessibility** unless noted.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Zoom** | Makes everything bigger or smaller. Also **Ctrl +**, **Ctrl −**, **Ctrl 0**, or Ctrl and scroll, anywhere in the app |
| **High contrast** | Stronger borders and text; turns off card fading |
| **Reduce motion** | No animations (also follows your device's own setting) |
| **Strong focus outline** | A thick ring around whatever the keyboard is on |
| **Larger click targets** | Bigger buttons, checkboxes and menu items |
| **Readable spacing** | Extra space between letters, words and lines |
| **Underline links** | Every link stands out without relying on color |

## Also helpful

- **Theme and density** (**Settings > Appearance**): dark or light, and compact to comfortable spacing.
- **Keyboard only:** everything can be reached with Tab, Enter and Esc, and cards can be moved with **Shift Space** and the arrow keys. See [Keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard).
- **Never color alone:** priorities, labels and late dates also have words or icons.
- **Spell check** works in every text field (on Linux, install a dictionary such as `hunspell-en_us`).
- **Phones and tablets:** each phone or tablet keeps its own look and accessibility settings, even while it shows your computer's boards.
