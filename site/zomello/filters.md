# Filters

A filter shows only the cards you care about right now, and hides the rest until you clear it. Nothing is moved or changed: the hidden cards are still there.

![Filter menu](/img/zomello/filter.png)

## Filter by words

Type in **Filter cards…** at the top of a board. Cards stay on screen when the words appear in their title, description, checklist, notes or file names. On a computer, press **/** to jump there.

## The Filter menu

**Filter** next to the box narrows the board further:

| Filter | Shows cards that are… |
|---|---|
| **Due** | **Late** (past their due date and not completed), due **Today**, due in the **Next 7 days** (today included), or have **No date** |
| **Progress** | Not started, In progress, Waiting, On hold or Completed |
| **Priority** | Urgent, Important, Medium or Low |
| **Labels** | Wearing any of the labels you tick |
| **Assigned to** | Assigned to anyone you tick, or **Unassigned** |

**How they combine:** ticking more than one thing in the same group shows cards that match **any** of them (Urgent *or* Important). Choices in different groups must **all** match (Urgent *and* labelled "Work" *and* due this week).

The button shows how many choices are on, like **Filter (3)**, and the toolbar shows how many cards match. **Clear** turns everything off.

## Saved filters

Use the same filter often? Set it up, then type a name under **Saved filters** at the top of the Filter menu and press **Save**. Next time, pick it from the same place. The **×** next to a saved filter deletes it (not the cards).

Saved filters belong to the board you made them on.

## Show a card's linked tasks

A card's menu (right-click it, or press and hold it on a phone) has **Show related tasks** when the card has sub-tasks, a parent card or linked tasks. The board then shows only that card and the cards linked to it.

- **Whole chain** follows the links all the way: sub-tasks of sub-tasks, and what those are linked to.
- The **×** on the "Related to…" chip goes back to the full board.

## Good to know

- **Every view is filtered:** Board, List, Calendar, Timeline and Charts all show only the matching cards, so Charts can count just one person's tasks, for example.
- **Each board remembers its own filter** until you clear it, even after closing Zomello. If a board looks emptier than it should, check for **Clear** in the toolbar.
- **Adding a card while a filter is on** works as usual. If the new card doesn't match the filter, it's hidden right away: clear the filter to see it.
- **Change all the matching cards at once:** with a filter on, **Ctrl A** selects every card it shows, then the bar at the bottom changes them together. See [Select several cards](./keyboard#select-several-cards).
- **Group by** (on the right of the toolbar) isn't a filter, but works well with one: group the filtered cards by person, priority, progress or due date instead of by bucket.

## Examples

- **What's on my plate this week:** Assigned to *you*, Due *Next 7 days*. Save it as "My week".
- **What's stuck:** Progress *Waiting* and *On hold*.
- **Overdue and important:** Due *Late*, Priority *Urgent* and *Important*.
- **Nobody's doing it:** Assigned to *Unassigned*, Progress *Not started*.

## Across all boards

Filters work on one board. To look across every board, use **Search** (**Ctrl K**), **My Tasks** (everything assigned to you) or **My Day** (what's late, due and planned today). See [Home, My Tasks and search](./home-and-search).
