# Getting started

This page gets you from installing Zomello to your first board in a few minutes.

## 1. Install it

| Device | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11** | Download `Zomello_…_x64-setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zomello/releases/latest) and open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info > Run anyway**. |
| **Linux** | Download the `.deb` (Ubuntu, Mint, Debian), `.rpm` (Fedora, openSUSE) or `.AppImage` (any) and open it. |
| **Android** | Install from Google Play, or download `Zomello.apk` from the release and open it. |
| **iPhone and iPad** | Install from the App Store. |

There's no account to create. Everything you make is saved on that device.

## 2. The welcome guide

The first time Zomello opens, it asks your name (so tasks assigned to you show up in **My Tasks**) and whether you like the light or dark look. You can change both later in **Settings > People** and **Settings > Appearance**.

A **Getting started** board is waiting for you. Each card on it shows one feature; open them in order, then delete the board when you're done.

## 3. Make your first board

1. In the menu on the left (on a phone, tap ☰), click **+ Board**.
2. Type a name, for example "Home Projects", and press **Enter**.
3. The board starts with three buckets: **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**. Rename one by clicking its title.
4. Click **+ Add card** under a bucket, type a task and press **Enter**.
5. Drag the card to **In progress**. Its progress changes for you.

![The menu with sections](/img/zomello/phone-menu.png){.phone}

## 4. Organize boards into sections

Sections group boards in the menu, like notebooks: Work, Home, School.

- Click **+ Section** at the bottom of the menu and name it.
- Drag boards into it, or press and hold (right-click on a computer) a board and choose **Category…**.

## 5. Useful from day one

- **Quick add:** press **N** (or the ⚡ **Quick add** button, or **+** on a phone) to add a task to any board from anywhere.
- **Search:** **Ctrl K** (or 🔍 on a phone) searches boards, cards, checklists and notes.
- **Right-click** (press and hold on touch screens) any card, bucket or board for all its options.
- **Undo:** **Ctrl Z**, or the **Undo** button on the message after you delete or archive something.
