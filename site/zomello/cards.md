# Cards

A card is one task. Click it to open it; everything saves as you type, and **Esc** (or clicking outside) closes it.

![An open card](/img/zomello/card.png)

## On the board

- **Add:** **+ Add card** under a bucket. Press **Enter** to add it and start the next; **Esc** to stop.
- **Move:** drag it to another bucket or position. On a phone, press and hold briefly, then drag.
- **Complete:** click the circle next to its title.
- **Right-click** (press and hold on a phone) for the card menu: progress, priority, labels, people, due date, repeat, reminders, timers, move, link, copy, template, archive and delete.

![The card menu](/img/zomello/card-menu.png)

## The main parts of a card

| Part | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Title** | Click to edit |
| **Mark complete** | Finishes it. Completed cards fold under **Show completed** |
| **Bucket** | Which column it's in |
| **Progress** | Not started, In progress, Waiting (on someone else), On hold (paused on purpose), Completed |
| **Priority** | Urgent, Important, Medium, Low. Every priority except Medium shows a colored flag on the card |
| **Start date / Due date** | Cards show "in 3 days", "today" or "2 days late" |
| **Assigned to** | People from **Settings > People**. Yours show on Home and in My Tasks |
| **Labels** | Colored tags. **Labels** at the top of the board manages them |
| **Description** | Longer details, with formatting (see below) |
| **Checklist** | Steps to tick off |
| **Notes** | Comments with dates, newest first |

The rest (repeating, reminders, sub-tasks, linked tasks, files, covers, time tracking, custom fields) is in [Cards in depth](./cards-in-depth).

## Labels

Labels are colored tags (Bug, Idea, Urgent, Groceries…) that belong to a board.

- **On a card:** open it > the **+** under Labels, then pick one, or type a new name to create it. The pencil next to a label edits it.
- **Manage a board's labels:** **Labels** at the top of the board. Rename a label, click its color to change it, delete it (it's removed from every card), or **Add label**.
- **How they look on cards:** named pills or thin color bars ([Customizing a board](./customize-board)).
- **Filter by label:** **Filter** at the top of the board, or **Group by > Label**.
- New boards start with Bug, Feature, Design, Research, Blocked and Idea; change them freely.

## Formatting text

The description and notes support **Markdown**, with a toolbar: bold (**Ctrl B**), italic (**Ctrl I**), strikethrough, heading, bulleted and numbered lists, checklist, quote, inline code (**Ctrl E**), code block, link (**Ctrl K**), table and divider.

- Checklist boxes inside a description can be ticked right in the finished text.
- Code blocks get a **Copy** button.
- Links open in your browser.

## Quick changes without opening the card

From the card menu: **Due date > Today / Tomorrow / Next week / In 2 weeks**, **Remind me > In 1 hour / Tomorrow 9:00…**, **Move to bucket**, **Move to board**, **Duplicate**, **Archive**.

On a computer, point at a card and press: **C** complete, **D** due today (press again for tomorrow), **A** archive, **Shift D** duplicate. More in [Keyboard shortcuts](./keyboard).
