# Coming from Trello or Planner

Bring your boards over in a minute. Imports always make a **new** board, so nothing you already have changes, and **Ctrl Z** undoes an import.

## 1. Export from your old app

| From | How |
|---|---|
| **Trello** | Open the board > **⋯ Menu** > **Print, export, and share** > **Export as JSON**. Save the page that opens as a `.json` file. |
| **Microsoft Planner** | Open the plan > **⋯** next to the plan's name > **Export plan to Excel**. Use the `.xlsx` file as it is (or save it as `.csv`). |

## 2. Import into Zomello

1. **Settings > Data > Import a board**.
2. Choose the file (or drop it onto that window). Zomello shows a preview: the board name (you can change it), how many buckets, cards, labels and people, and each bucket with its card count.
3. Tick **Add (people) to Zomello** to keep cards assigned to the same names.
4. Click **Import as a new board**.

## What comes along

Buckets (lists), cards, descriptions, checklists, labels, due and start dates, people, progress and archived cards. Anything Zomello can't bring over is listed as a note in the preview.

## Afterwards

- Put the board in a section, star it, or [customize it](./customize-board).
- Check **Settings > People**: rename people to how you want them shown (it updates every card).
