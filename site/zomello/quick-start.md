# Quick start

Everything you need for your first day with Zomello, in about ten minutes. When you want more, the [advanced features](./cards-in-depth) and [set-up scenarios](./scenarios/) are next.

## 1. Install Zomello

Full details for each system, updating and uninstalling: [Installing and updating](./installing).

| Device | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11** | Download `Zomello_…_x64-setup.exe` from the [latest release](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zomello/releases/latest) and open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info > Run anyway**. |
| **Linux** | Download the `.deb` (Ubuntu, Mint, Debian), `.rpm` (Fedora, openSUSE) or `.AppImage` (any) and open it. |
| **Android** | Download `Zomello.apk` from the release and open it (Google Play coming soon). |
| **iPhone and iPad** | The App Store (coming soon). |
| **Just looking?** | Try the [online demo](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zomello/) in your browser. Nothing to install. |

There's no account to make and nothing to sign up for. Your boards are saved on the device you're using.

## 2. Say hello

The first time Zomello opens, a short welcome guide asks:

- **Your name.** Tasks assigned to you then show up on **Home** and in **My Tasks**.
- **Light, dark, or match your device.**

You can change both later in **Settings > People** and **Settings > Appearance**. A **Getting started** board is waiting with one feature per card: open them in order, then delete the board (right-click it in the menu > **Delete**).

## 3. Find your way around

Lost at any point? **Help** at the bottom of the menu shows the welcome tour again and links back to this guide.

![Zomello's main window](/img/zomello/board.png)

- **The menu (left side):** Home, My Tasks, your boards (grouped in sections), the focus timer, Archive, Settings and Help. On a phone, tap the **menu button** (three lines, top left) to open it.
- **The board (middle):** buckets side by side, cards inside them.
- **The board's top bar:** the board's name, the **views** (Board, List, Calendar, Timeline, Charts), **Labels**, **Customize** and **⋯** for more.

::: tip Right-click everything
Cards, buckets, boards and sections all have a menu with every option. On a phone or tablet, **press and hold** instead.
:::

## 4. Make a board

1. Click **+ Board** at the bottom of the menu.
2. Type a name, for example "Home Projects", and press **Enter**.
3. The board starts with three buckets: **To do**, **In progress** and **Done**. Click a bucket's title to rename it, or **+ Add bucket** to add more.

## 5. Add and move cards

1. Under a bucket, click **+ Add card**, type the task and press **Enter**. Keep typing to add more; press **Esc** when you're done.
2. **Drag** a card to another bucket. Dropping it in **In progress** or **Done** updates its progress for you.
3. **Click a card** to open it. Add a due date, a checklist, labels, people, notes or files. Everything saves as you type.
4. **Mark complete** at the top of the card (or click the circle on the card itself). Completed cards fold away under **Show completed** at the bottom of the bucket.

## 6. Group your boards into sections

Sections are like notebooks in the menu: Work, Home, School.

1. Click **+ Section** at the bottom of the menu and name it.
2. Drag boards into it. (Or right-click a board > **Category…**.)
3. The **+** next to a section's name makes a new board right inside it.

## 7. See what's due

- **Home** shows what's late, due today, coming up this week and in progress, across every board.
- **My Tasks** lists everything assigned to you.
- **Calendar** (on any board) shows cards by due date; drag a card to another day to move it.

## 8. Five habits that save time

| Do this | How |
|---|---|
| Add a task from anywhere | **Quick add** (press **N** on a computer, **+** at the top on a phone) |
| Find anything | **Search** (**Ctrl K**, or the magnifier on a phone) |
| Undo a mistake | **Ctrl Z**, or **Undo** on the message that appears |
| Get a reminder | Open a card > **Reminder**, or right-click > **Remind me** |
| Repeat a chore | Open a card > **Repeat** (daily, weekdays, weekly…) |
