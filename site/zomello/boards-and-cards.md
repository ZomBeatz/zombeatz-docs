# Boards, buckets and cards

Everything in Zomello lives on a **board**. A board has **buckets** (columns), and buckets hold **cards** (tasks).

![A board with four buckets](/img/zomello/board.png)

## Boards

- **New board:** **+ Board** in the menu, or the **+** next to a section's name to make it in that section.
- **Rename:** double-click its name in the menu, or right-click > **Rename**.
- **Star** a board to pin it at the top of the menu.
- **Customize…** (right-click, or the 🎨 button on the board): icon, color, background (colors, gradients or your own picture), column width, compact cards, which details show on cards, and custom fields.
- **Duplicate** copies a board with its cards; **New board with this layout** copies only the buckets and settings.
- **Archive** hides a board (find it again under **Archive**); **Delete** removes it, with **Undo** right after.
- **Private: only on this device** keeps a board from being shown on your other devices. See [Sharing between your devices](./sharing-between-devices).

### Sections

Sections group boards in the menu. Drag a board into a section, or right-click > **Category…**. A section stays when its last board leaves; right-click a section's name to rename or remove it.

## Buckets

- **Add:** **+ Add bucket** at the end of the board.
- **Move:** drag a bucket by its title.
- The bucket's **⋯** menu:
  - **Cards moved here become…**: for example, cards dropped in "Done" become **Completed** automatically.
  - **Sort by** due date, priority, name, progress, newest or recently updated. Sorting never changes your own order underneath.
  - **WIP limit**: a "work in progress" limit; the bucket warns when it holds more cards.
  - **Copy work order** and **Paste cards here…** (see [Copying and pasting cards](./copy-paste-cards)).
- Completed cards fold into **Show completed** at the bottom of each bucket.

## Cards

Click a card to open it. Everything saves as you type.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Mark complete** | At the top. Completed cards fold away under their bucket |
| **Progress** | Not started, In progress, Waiting, On hold, Completed |
| **Priority** | Urgent, Important, Medium, Low |
| **Dates** | Start and due date. Cards show "in 3 days" or "1 day late" |
| **Repeat** | Daily, weekly on chosen days, monthly or yearly, every N, until a date. Completing it makes the next one |
| **Reminder** | A notification at the time you pick |
| **People** | Assign cards; yours show in **My Tasks**. Add people in **Settings > People** |
| **Labels** | Colored tags; manage them with **Labels** on the board |
| **Description** | Markdown with a formatting toolbar; code blocks get a **Copy** button |
| **Checklist** | Items you tick off. An item can link to another card, or turn into its own card ("sub-task") |
| **Linked tasks** | Related, blocks or blocked by. Blocked cards show a badge |
| **Files and links** | Attach files (button or drag them onto the card) or paste a link. Pictures can be the card's cover |
| **Notes** | A running log of comments with dates |
| **Time** | Start and pause a timer, log time by hand, set an estimate |
| **Activity** | Every change to the card, with when it happened |

### Card shortcuts (on a computer)

Point at a card and press: **Enter** open, **C** complete, **D** due today (again for tomorrow), **T** start or pause its timer, **F** start a focus countdown, **A** archive, **Shift D** duplicate. **Ctrl click** selects several cards for bulk actions.

## Templates

Right-click a card > **Save as template**. Use it from **+ Add card** or **Quick add**. Manage templates in **Settings > Templates**.
