# Templates

Templates are ready-made cards for things you do often: a bug report, a weekly review, a packing list.

## Save a template

Open a card that looks the way you want (title, description, checklist, labels, priority, repeat) and choose **Save as template** (also in its right-click menu).

## Use a template

- The **template button** (a small layout icon) next to **+ Add card** in any bucket.
- In **Quick add**, pick it from the template list.

The new card gets everything from the template. A repeating template keeps its repeat rule and sets its own first due date.

## Manage templates

**Settings > Templates** lists them with how many checklist items and labels each has. Click a name to rename it; the trash deletes it.

## Board templates

For a whole board layout (buckets, labels, fields, look), use **New board with this layout** on an existing board. It copies everything except the cards.
