# Copying and pasting cards

Copy cards from one board and paste them into another, on the same device or a different one.

## Copy

- **One card:** right-click (press and hold) the card > **Copy card**.
- **A bucket's open cards:** the bucket's **⋯** > **Copy work order**.
- **A whole board's open cards:** the board's **⋯** > **Copy work order (all open cards)**.
- **Several cards:** **Ctrl click** them, then **Copy work order** in the bar that appears.

The copy is readable text, so you can also paste it into a note, an email or an AI chat.

## Paste

1. Open the board you want them on.
2. The board's **⋯** > **Paste cards…**, or a bucket's **⋯** > **Paste cards here…**.
3. Pick the bucket under **Into**. The cards box fills in from the clipboard when the device allows it; otherwise press and hold in the box and choose **Paste**.
4. Check the preview ("3 cards: …") and click **Add 3 cards**.

On a computer you can also just press **Ctrl V** while looking at a board.

Pasted cards keep their titles, descriptions, checklists, labels, priority, dates and people. People who aren't in your list yet are added.

## Paste a list from anywhere

A plain list works too: each line becomes a card, and indented lines become its checklist.

```
- Groceries
  - Milk
  - Eggs
- Call the plumber
```

## Between devices

Copy on one device and paste on another if your devices share a clipboard (for example Windows and Android with Phone Link, or KDE Connect on Linux). Inside the phone app, copy from your computer's boards and paste into the phone's own boards: they share the phone's clipboard.

To move a whole board instead, see [Moving boards between devices](./moving-boards).
