# Customizing a board

Each board can look and behave its own way. Open a board and click **Customize** at the top (or press **B**, or right-click the board > **Customize…**).

![The Customize window](/img/zomello/customize.png)

## Icon and color

- **Icon:** pick one of the suggested icons or type any emoji. It shows in the menu, on Home and in the board's title.
- **Board color:** used for the bucket tops, the dot in the menu and the charts.

## About this board

A few lines saying what the board is for: the project, who's involved, where things live. It's included when you use the [AI tools](./ai-tools) or export the board, so you don't have to explain it again.

## Background

- Choose a **color**, a **gradient**, or **Upload an image** (or drop a picture onto this section).
- **Background effects:** **Blur** softens a busy picture; **Dim** darkens it so cards stay readable.

## Layout

- **Column width:** narrow, normal or wide buckets.
- **Labels on cards:** **Named pills** (the label's name in color) or **Color bars** (thin colored stripes, more compact).
- **Compact cards:** tighter spacing and smaller text, to fit more on screen.

## What cards show

For each kind of detail, choose **Hidden**, **Icon** (a small symbol or count) or **Details** (the actual information):

| Detail | Icon | Details |
|---|---|---|
| **Dates** | Due date | Dates plus "in 3 days" or "2 days late", and the repeat rule |
| **Priority** | Colored flag | Named badge (Urgent, Important…) |
| **Checklist** | Count (2/5) | The items themselves, tickable right on the card |
| **Assigned to** | Avatars | Avatars with names |
| **Description** | Icon | The first lines |
| **Notes** | Count | The latest note |
| **Linked tasks** | Count | Linked task names |
| **Files & links** | Count | File names |
| **Time tracked** | Total | Total and estimate |

**Hide ticked checklist items on cards** keeps finished steps out of the way when the checklist shows details. A single bucket can override all of this from its menu (**Card display…**, **Ticked checklist items**).

## Card details window

Untick parts of the card window you don't use on this board: Labels, Custom fields, Activity, Progress, Start date, Due date, Reminder, Repeat, Time & focus, Cover, Move to board. Nothing is deleted; tick it again to bring it back.

## Card aging

**Fade cards nobody has touched in a while.** Unfinished cards fade a little after 1, 2 and 4 weeks without any change, so forgotten work stands out. Editing, moving or adding a note makes a card fresh again.

## Calendars

Show events from Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar in this board's Calendar view. See [Calendar feeds](./calendar-feeds).

## Category

Which section the board is in. Same as dragging it in the menu.

## Custom fields

Extra information on every card: see [Custom fields](./custom-fields).
