## About ZomBeatz Media

ZomBeatz Media makes practical tools that run on your own computers, phones and servers. They share a few promises:

- **Your data stays yours.** No accounts with us, no ads, no tracking. Nothing is sent to ZomBeatz Media.
- **No AI inside.** Where a tool can help you use an AI assistant, it only prepares text you copy yourself, and you never have to.
- **Works on what you have.** Windows, Linux, phones and tablets, and your own server or Docker when you want to share.
- **One family.** The tools look and work alike, so learning one helps with the next.
