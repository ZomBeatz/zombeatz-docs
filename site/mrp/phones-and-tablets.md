# Phones and tablets

Zom's MRP is laid out for small screens. Open your team's [server](./server) in the phone's browser and sign in, or try the [live demo](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zoms_MRP/) on the phone.

![The Overview and the menu on a phone](/img/mrp/phone-menu.png)

## Getting around

- **☰** at the top left opens the menu of pages. **+** at the top right starts a new mission, and the magnifier finds anything.
- Lists are cards instead of tables, and the summary tiles sit two to a row.
- The phone's back button steps back through what you opened: from a season to its mission, then to the page before.

## Charts

Charts are wider than a phone. Swipe sideways to move through time, and pinch to zoom.

![The Fleet page on a phone](/img/mrp/phone-fleet.png)

## Forms

Forms open full screen. Their tabs are along the top, **Save** and **Cancel** along the bottom. The back button closes a form without saving, and asks first if you've changed something.

![A season's pilots on a phone](/img/mrp/phone-season.png)

## Tablets

On a tablet, missions, crew, maintenance and training show as cards two across. A phone turned sideways keeps the phone layout.

## Adding it to the home screen

In the phone's browser menu, choose **Add to Home screen** (Android) or **Share > Add to Home Screen** (iPhone and iPad). It then opens like an app.
