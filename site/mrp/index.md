# Zom's MRP

**Zom's MRP** (Mission Ready Planner) schedules helicopter work: aircraft by tail number, pilots and maintainers rotating through hitches, maintenance, inspections and training. Think of a fleet board, a crew roster and a Gantt chart in one, with the checks built in.

It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, or on your own server for a whole team. There's no account and no cloud: the plan stays on your computer or your server.

## Start here

- **New?** [Quick start](./quick-start) takes you from download to your first crewed mission in about 15 minutes.
- **Just looking?** [The live demo](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zoms_MRP/) has 12 helicopters, about 45 crew and 19 missions, with lessons in its Guide.
- **How many pilots does a job take?** The [Crew planner](./crew-planner) works it out, and the [set-up scenarios](./scenarios/) walk through real examples.
- **Want everything?** The basics pages cover the everyday things, the advanced pages cover every option, and [Settings](./settings) lists every setting.

## What you can do

| | |
|---|---|
| **See what the fleet can support** | Every tail over time, its free windows, and **Can we take a job?** before saying yes ([Fleet](./fleet)) |
| **Plan each customer's work** | A mission per customer, a season per stretch of dates, with aircraft and crew sizes ([Missions and seasons](./missions-and-seasons)) |
| **Rotate crews through hitches** | 14 on, 7 off or any rotation, filled with rated, current, rested people ([Filling the schedule](./filling-the-schedule)) |
| **Know how many pilots a job takes** | The fewest pilots and the rotation that does it, with the arithmetic shown ([Crew planner](./crew-planner)) |
| **Keep to the rules** | Days on and off, duty days, rest, flight-hour limits and kinds of contract ([Work limits](./work-limits)) |
| **See every problem first** | Open seats, double bookings, missing ratings, lapsed training, too-long hitches, each with a fix ([Overview and problems](./overview)) |
| **Track training** | Courses set per mission, who's current, and warnings before anything runs out ([Training](./training)) |
| **Plan maintenance** | Jobs that ground a tail, inspections by hours or months, flight hours ([Maintenance and inspections](./maintenance)) |
| **Spread the work fairly** | How busy everyone is, and a rebalance that keeps to every rule ([Balance](./balance)) |
| **Share it** | PDF and Excel reports, a week sheet for the crew room, an Excel copy for Teams ([Reports and sharing](./reports)) |
| **Work as a team** | One plan on your own server, with Admin, Scheduler and Viewer accounts ([Setting up a server](./server)) |

## Where it runs

| Device | How |
|---|---|
| **Windows 10/11** | An installer from [the latest release](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zoms_MRP/releases/latest) ([Installing and updating](./installing)) |
| **Mac** | One app for Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 10.15 or later |
| **Linux** | `.deb`, `.rpm` or AppImage |
| **A server** | Docker with https, for a team signing in from their browsers ([Setting up a server](./server)) |
| **Phones and tablets** | The server's app in the browser, laid out for a small screen ([Phones and tablets](./phones-and-tablets)) |
