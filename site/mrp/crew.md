# Crew and time off

The **Crew** page lists your pilots and maintainers. Tap someone to see their details, ratings, training and what they're booked on next.

![A pilot's form beside the crew list](/img/mrp/person.png)

## A person's tabs

| Tab | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Name, role, base, phone, **Day rate**, their own fixed rotation, own **Work limits**, notes, and **Booked next** |
| **Ratings and missions** | The aircraft types they're rated on, when they last flew each type, and the missions they're on |
| **Training** | One line per course, with when it runs out |

### A fixed rotation of their own

Tick **Works a fixed rotation** for someone on a set pattern, like 21 on, 14 off from a first day. They're only offered on their on days, their off days are shaded on the [Schedule](./schedule), and booking them on one is flagged.

### Their schedule as a PDF

**Their schedule (PDF)** on a person makes their next 90 days on a page: shifts, handovers, notes, maintenance, time off and training.

## Time off

1. On **Crew**, press **Add time off…**.
2. Pick who, the first and last day, and a reason.
3. Press **Add time off**.

Any shift they had on those days shows under **Needs attention** on the Overview. **Swap them out** opens the shift, ready to pick someone else.

Tick **Only a preference** for days someone would rather not work. They can still be booked and it's never a problem, but Fill and Rebalance pick someone else when they can. It shows lighter on the Schedule.

## Trading and handing over shifts

1. Open the person and, under **Booked next**, press **Trade…** beside the shift.
2. **Hand it to someone** lists who can legally take it, the mission's crew first. **Trade with someone** lists two-way trades where each can take the other's shift.
3. Tap one. Undo is in the message.

## Currency

A pilot is current on a type if they've flown it within the days set in Settings (90 to start). It comes from their shifts, or a **Last flew** date on **Ratings and missions** for flying done elsewhere. Booking someone who isn't current is a warning, and Fill won't do it.
