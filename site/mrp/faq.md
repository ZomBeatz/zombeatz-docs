# Questions

Short answers to what people ask most, each with a link to the full story.

## Is Zom's MRP free?

Yes. It's free and open source under the MIT license, with no ads and no paid tier. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zoms_MRP).

## Do I need an account or the internet?

No. The desktop app works offline with no sign-up. Only a [server](./server) has accounts, and those are made by your own admin.

## Where is my plan kept? Does ZomBeatz Media see it?

On your computer in the desktop app, or in your server's database. Nothing is sent to ZomBeatz Media. More in [Data and backups](./data-and-backups).

## Is there any AI in it?

No. Every number comes from plain arithmetic you can see, like the [Crew planner](./crew-planner#why-that-many)'s "Why" list.

## Can I use it on a phone?

Yes, through a [server](./server) in the phone's browser. The layout is made for small screens. More in [Phones and tablets](./phones-and-tablets).

## How does it decide someone can't fly a job?

They must be rated on one of its aircraft types, current on the mission's training, free, not on time off, on their own rotation if they have one, and inside the [work limits](./work-limits). [Filling the schedule](./filling-the-schedule#who-it-picks) has the full list.

## How many pilots does a job need?

Put the job in the [Crew planner](./crew-planner). It gives the fewest pilots for your rules and the rotation that does it.

## Can it follow flight-hour limits like 100 hours in 30 days?

Yes, as many limits as you need, set in **Settings > Hitches and days off**. See [Work limits](./work-limits#flight-hour-limits).

## How do I share the plan in Teams?

Make a **PDF report** or **Excel report** on Reports, or let the desktop app keep an Excel copy in a Teams folder. See [Reports and sharing](./reports).

## Can I bring in my existing spreadsheets?

Yes: people, aircraft, courses and training records, from Excel or CSV. See [Importing from Excel or CSV](./data-and-backups#importing-from-excel-or-csv).

## How do I get my data out?

**Settings > Your data > Save a backup** (to restore anywhere) or **Export to Excel** (to read anywhere).

## Where do I find help inside the app?

**Guide** in the sidebar (or <kbd>?</kbd>) explains every page, with step-by-step **How do I…** answers, and each section links to its page here. **Find** (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) finds pages and how-tos as well as people and tails. A new page shows a short note on where to start for its first few visits.

## What's new in the latest version?

See [What's new](./whats-new).

## How do I report a bug or ask for something?

**Settings > About > Send feedback**, or email [info@zombeatzmedia.com](mailto:info@zombeatzmedia.com). See [Contact and feedback](./contact).
