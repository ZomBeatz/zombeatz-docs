# Balance

**Balance** shows how busy everyone is over a period, and lets you try other crew sizes and spread the work again. Nothing changes until you press **Apply**.

![The Balance page](/img/mrp/balance.png)

## How busy everyone is

Pick **Next 30 days**, **Next 90 days** or **Next 6 months**. Each person's bar is the share of the days they can work (time off doesn't count) that they're booked:

| Color | Means |
|---|---|
| Red | Very busy |
| Green | Busy |
| Blue | Light |
| Grey | Hardly used |

The tiles show open seat-days, conflicts, serious conflicts and the busiest person.

## Trying it another way

1. Under **Jobs in this period**, change a job's pilots or maintainers, say from 2 to 1. Nothing is saved yet.
2. **Lock** any job or person that should stay as it is.
3. Press **Rebalance**. It keeps the shifts that are there, fills open seats, and starts over for the jobs whose size you changed. Tick **Start over for every job** to redo them all.
4. Compare before and after: the numbers at the top, and a line on each person's bar where they are now.
5. Press **Apply** to keep it (Undo is in the message), or **Undo the changes here** to drop it.

Time off, ratings, training, rotations, rest and flight-hour limits are always followed.
