# Words used in Zom's MRP

| Word | Means |
|---|---|
| **Balance** | How busy everyone is, and rebalancing the work ([Balance](./balance)) |
| **Conflict** | Someone or something booked when they can't be, like twice at once ([Overview and problems](./overview)) |
| **Crew** | A mission's regular people, or all pilots and maintainers ([Crew and time off](./crew)) |
| **Crew planner** | Works out how few pilots a job takes ([Crew planner](./crew-planner)) |
| **Current** | Training that hasn't run out, or a pilot who flew a type recently enough ([Training](./training)) |
| **Duty day** | The hours someone is on duty in a day ([Work limits](./work-limits)) |
| **Extended** | Training counted as current past its date, with a reason ([Training](./training#extending-a-course)) |
| **Flight-hour limit** | Most flight hours per pilot in any so many days ([Work limits](./work-limits#flight-hour-limits)) |
| **Grounded** | An aircraft that can't fly, usually for maintenance ([Maintenance and inspections](./maintenance)) |
| **Hitch** | Days someone works in a row before time off. Shifts back to back count as one. ([Work limits](./work-limits)) |
| **Kind of contract** | Named days on and off, like EMS or fire, a mission can use ([Work limits](./work-limits#kinds-of-contract)) |
| **Mission** | One customer's kind of work ([Missions and seasons](./missions-and-seasons)) |
| **Night seat** | A seat on a 24-hour aircraft that needs a night-qualified pilot ([Crew planner](./crew-planner#night-seats)) |
| **Open seat** | A day a job needs one more pilot or maintainer than it has ([Overview and problems](./overview)) |
| **Rated** | Allowed to fly or work on a type of aircraft ([Crew and time off](./crew)) |
| **Rest** | Hours off between two duty days ([Work limits](./work-limits)) |
| **Rotation** | A repeating pattern of days on and off, like 14 on, 7 off ([Filling the schedule](./filling-the-schedule)) |
| **Season** | One stretch of dates a mission flies ([Missions and seasons](./missions-and-seasons)) |
| **Shift** | One person on a season for some days in a row ([Schedule](./schedule)) |
| **Sub** | Someone from outside a mission's crew covering an open seat ([Filling the schedule](./filling-the-schedule#crewing-from-the-mission-s-own-people)) |
| **Tail** | An aircraft, named by its tail number ([Fleet](./fleet)) |
| **Travel days** | Days getting to and from a job, counted as days on ([Crew planner](./crew-planner)) |
| **Type** | The kind of aircraft, like Bell 412 ([Fleet](./fleet)) |
