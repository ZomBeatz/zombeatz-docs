# Settings

Every setting, in the same order as **Settings** in the app.

![The Settings page](/img/mrp/settings.png)

## Server (server only)

On a server, admins see the people with access, the activity log and backups first. See [People and roles](./server-people) and [Backups and security](./server-backups). Everyone can **Change password** there.

## Appearance

- **Match my computer**, **Light** or **Dark**.

## Preferences

- **Week starts on:** **Monday** or **Sunday**.
- **Currency symbol:** shown before amounts in [costs](./costs).
- **Pages in the menu:** untick pages you don't use (Fleet, Schedule, Missions, Crew, Maintenance, Training, Balance, Crew planner, Reports) to keep the menu short. Inspections has its own switch.
- **New seasons start with:** the pilots, maintainers and rotation of a mission's first season. Later seasons start from the one before. A rotation of 0 on uses the hitch rules.

## Hitches and days off

- **Most days on in a row**, **Fewest days off between hitches**.
- **Most hours on duty in a day**, **Rest between duty days**.
- **Most flight hours:** one or more limits, like 100 in 30.
- **Current on a type:** days since a pilot last flew it.
- **Warn before training runs out:** days ahead.

All of them are explained in [Work limits](./work-limits).

## Kinds of contract

- Named sets of days on and off, such as EMS or fire, that a mission can pick. **Add a kind of contract**; the bin removes one, and its missions go back to the rules above. See [Kinds of contract](./work-limits#kinds-of-contract).

## Maintenance programs

- **Track inspections and flight hours here:** turns inspections on or off.
- **Plan inspections automatically:** books due inspections when the app opens.
- **Flight hours on a mission day:** for tails without their own.
- **Due soon:** days ahead.

See [Maintenance and inspections](./maintenance#inspections).

## Your data

Called **The plan** on a server.

- **Open folder** (desktop app): shows where the plan is kept.
- **Save a backup:** a file of the whole plan.
- **Import from Excel or CSV…** and **Download the import template**.
- **Export to Excel:** the whole plan as a workbook.
- **Restore a backup…:** replaces the plan with a backup (admins only on a server).
- **Clear all data…:** deletes everything, after you type DELETE.

See [Data and backups](./data-and-backups).

## Excel copy for Teams (desktop app only)

- **Choose a folder…** to keep an Excel copy there, with **Write it now**, **Show it** and **Stop keeping a copy**. See [Reports and sharing](./reports#an-excel-copy-for-teams-desktop-app).
