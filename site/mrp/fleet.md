# Fleet

The **Fleet** page shows what the fleet can support: every tail over time, grouped by type, with its missions, its maintenance and the free windows in between. Tap a free window to book it.

![The Fleet page with free windows in green](/img/mrp/fleet.png)

## Reading the board

| What you see | Means |
|---|---|
| Colored bars | Missions, each in its own color and pattern |
| Green dashed blocks | Free windows, counted from today |
| The thin row under a tail | Its maintenance. A job that grounds it is outlined red if a mission is booked on top. |
| **Free aircraft each day** at the top | How many tails are free each day. A red mark means none. |
| The line down the chart | The start of today |

The tiles above it show how many tails are free today, how booked the fleet is over the next 90 days, and free aircraft-days.

### Moving around

Pick **Week**, **2 weeks**, **Month**, **Quarter** or **Year**, or use the slider. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + scroll (or pinch) zooms, and dragging or <kbd>Shift</kbd> + scroll moves through time. **Today** jumps back.

## Can we take a job?

When a customer calls, check here before saying yes.

1. Press **Can we take a job?**.
2. Pick the first and last day, the **Aircraft type** (or any), the **Pilots on board** and any training the crew need.
3. Read the answer, tail by tail: **Can do**, **Short crew** or **Can't**, with the reason and the first day a busy tail is free.
4. Press **Book on…** beside a tail to start the mission with it all filled in.

![Can we take a job? listing each tail with whether it can take the dates](/img/mrp/fleet-can-we.png)

It checks the tails, and also whether enough rated, current, free pilots exist for those dates. For how many pilots the whole job takes over its run, use the [Crew planner](./crew-planner).

## Adding and changing aircraft

Press **Add aircraft…**. Each aircraft has:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Tail number** | How it's named everywhere, like N412ZM |
| **Type** | Like Bell 412. Pilot and maintainer ratings match this. |
| **Status** | **Ready**, **In maintenance**, **Grounded** or **Retired** |
| **Pilot seats** | 1 or 2. Booking more pilots than this is flagged. |
| **Base** | Where it's kept |
| **Cost per flight hour** | Optional, for [costs](./costs) |
| **Hours on a mission day** | Its flight hours on a mission day, for inspections and flight limits. Empty uses Settings. |

The aircraft's form also lists every pilot and maintainer to rate on its type with a tap.
