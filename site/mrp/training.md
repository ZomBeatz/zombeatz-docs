# Training

Each mission sets the training its crew need, so people only need the courses of the missions they work or are booked on. The **Training** page shows where everyone stands.

![The Training page](/img/mrp/training.png)

## Courses

Press **Add training…** to add a course. Give it a name and **Renew every (months)**, or 0 if it never runs out. Then tick it on each mission that needs it, on the mission's **Training** tab.

## Who's current

**Who's current** shows everyone against every course they need: current, runs out soon, or lapsed. Narrow it to one mission's crew. **Needs training** lists only the people with something missing, run out or running out, or who aren't current on a type, each opening right at the fix.

Warnings start the number of days set in **Settings > Hitches and days off** before a course runs out (**Warn before training runs out**). Any shift where training would lapse mid-hitch is flagged.

## Recording training

### For one person

1. Tap the person (on Training or Crew) and open their **Training** tab.
2. Tap the course, or press **Add training** if it isn't there.
3. Set **Completed** to the day they did it (**Today** fills in today). **Runs out** moves on by itself.
4. Press **Done**, then **Save**.

### For a class

1. Press **Record for several people…** (or open the course and press **Record it for several people…**).
2. Pick the course and the day, then tick who attended. **Tick everyone who needs it** does it in one go.

## Extending a course

When someone can't get back for a renewal, press **Extend it** on the training problem. Pick the day it counts as current until and write why, like "On the North Slope job until June". It shows **Extended** with the reason. Only use it where your operation allows.

## Night qualification in the Crew planner

The [Crew planner](./crew-planner#night-seats) can ask that night pilots hold a course, such as night vision goggles. Pick which course means "night qualified" there.
