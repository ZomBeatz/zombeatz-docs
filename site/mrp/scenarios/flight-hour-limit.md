# A 100 hours in 30 days limit

Your pilots may fly at most 100 hours in any 30 days. Two aircraft each fly about 6 hours a day for six months. Here flight hours, not days off, set how many pilots you need.

## Setting the limit (once)

1. Open **Settings > Hitches and days off**.
2. Under **Most flight hours**, press **Add a limit** and set 100 hours in 30 days. Add others the same way, like 1,000 in 365.

From then on, a pilot who would go over is flagged, and **Fill the schedule** and **Rebalance** keep under it. See [Flight-hour limits](../work-limits#flight-hour-limits).

## Working it out

1. Open **Crew planner**, **One job**, and put in the six months.
2. Add two aircraft at **12** hours staffed, with **Flight hours a day** at **6**.

The answer is **4 pilots**, and the **Why** list says **Everyone can only fly so much**:

- Each aircraft flies 6 × 30 = 180 hours in 30 days. At 100 each, that takes 1.8 pilots per aircraft, so 2 × 1.8 = 3.6, rounded up to **4**.
- At 8 hours a day each, it takes **5**.

## Every day

- Planned hours are the aircraft's flight hours a day, shared by its pilots.
- Once a shift is flown, type the logbook hours in its **Flight hours flown**, so the limit counts what really happened.

## Good to know

- "Any 30 days" means every window, not calendar months.
- Days-off rules and flight limits both apply. The planner says which one sets the number.
- Each aircraft's flight hours a day come from its **Hours on a mission day**, or **Settings > Maintenance programs**.
