# Moving in from a spreadsheet

Your crew, aircraft and training records are in spreadsheets today. You end up with them in Zom's MRP in a few minutes, ready for missions.

## On the computer (once)

1. Open **Settings > Your data** and press **Download the import template**.
2. Copy your lists into its **People**, **Aircraft**, **Courses** and **Training** sheets, keeping the headings. Any of them can be left empty. Hover a heading for what goes in it; the **Read me** sheet has examples.
3. Press **Import from Excel or CSV…** and pick the file.
4. Check the preview: what's added, what's updated (matched by name, or tail number) and what's skipped, with why.
5. Press **Import**. Undo works right after.

More in [Importing from Excel or CSV](../data-and-backups#importing-from-excel-or-csv).

## Then

1. Look at **Needs attention** on the Overview for missing ratings or lapsed courses.
2. Add each customer as a mission ([Missions and seasons](../missions-and-seasons)), with the training it needs.
3. Crew each season with **Fill the schedule** ([Filling the schedule](../filling-the-schedule)).

## Good to know

- Importing again updates what's there instead of adding twice, so you can fix the sheet and import it again.
- A CSV file holds one list; the template holds all four.
- Missions and seasons aren't imported. They're quick to add, and **Copy to next season** does next year.
