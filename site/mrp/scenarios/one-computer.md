# One scheduler, one computer

You do the scheduling on one computer, and the rest of the team only needs to see the plan. You end up with the desktop app, its daily copies, and the plan in Teams.

## On the computer (once)

1. [Install Zom's MRP](../installing) and follow the **Getting started** steps on the Overview ([Quick start](../quick-start)).
2. In Teams, open your crew channel's **Files** and choose **Sync**, so OneDrive keeps that folder on this computer.
3. In **Settings > Excel copy for Teams**, press **Choose a folder…** and pick it ([Reports and sharing](../reports#an-excel-copy-for-teams-desktop-app)).

## Every day

- Start on the **Overview** and clear **Needs attention**.
- Plan and crew in Zom's MRP. The Excel copy in Teams updates a few seconds after each change.
- Each week, make a **Week sheet** on **Reports** for the crew room.

## Good to know

- The app keeps a copy every day for a month, but they're on the same computer. Now and then, **Save a backup** somewhere else, like OneDrive ([Data and backups](../data-and-backups)).
- When a second scheduler joins, move to [a server](./team-server): save a backup and restore it there.
- Crew can't change anything in the Excel copy; it's for looking.
