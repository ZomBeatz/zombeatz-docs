# Missions and seasons

A **mission** is one customer's kind of work, like "Interior Fire Service". A **season** is one stretch of dates it flies. Go to **Missions** and tap one to open it.

![A mission's seasons, each with how well it's crewed and its status](/img/mrp/mission.png)

## Missions keep what stays the same

A mission's tabs:

| Tab | What's on it |
|---|---|
| **Details** | Name, contact, location, **Kind of contract**, **Charge per day**, its color and pattern on the charts, and its own **Work limits** |
| **Seasons** | Its stretches of dates, with how well each is crewed and its status |
| **Training** | The courses its crew need. Only people on this mission need them. |
| **Crew** | The people who usually work it. They get first pick when the schedule is filled. |
| **Rotations** | Each running or coming season's hitches in order: who, when, handovers and open seats |

Names don't need a year: that's what seasons are for. Each mission's color and pattern keep it apart from the others, even printed in grey.

## Seasons hold the dates

Open a season from the mission's **Seasons** tab, or tap its bar on the [Schedule](./schedule).

![A season's Details tab](/img/mrp/season.png)

On **Details**:

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Status** | **Planned**, **Confirmed**, **Flying**, **Done** or **Cancelled**. A planned one has **Confirm** at the top. |
| **First day**, **Last day** | Whole days. Tap the calendar to pick them. |
| **Aircraft** | Tap tails to add them, grouped by type, each saying whether it's free or what it's busy with. Add them whenever they're known. |
| **Pilots needed**, **Maintainers needed** | How many at a time, across all its aircraft |
| When maintainers are needed | **All the time**, **Days a week**, **Start and end** (set-up and tear-down) or **On call** (never a gap) |
| **Pilot rotation**, **Maintainer rotation** | Like 14 on, 7 off. They can differ. |
| **Hours on duty in a day** | Optional. Longer than allowed, or leaving too little rest, is flagged. |
| **Work limits** | This season's own days on and off, with **Override** ([Work limits](./work-limits)) |

**Pilots** and **Maintainers** show how much is crewed, then one line per shift with open seats in date order. **Problems** lists this season's problems and fixes them in place. A red dot on a tab means something there needs a look.

## Everyday jobs

### A customer wants more days

1. Open the season and change the **Last day** (or first day) on **Details**.
2. On **Pilots**, press **Fill the schedule** to crew the new days. Do the same on **Maintainers**.
3. Press **Save**.

### Book the same job next year

On the mission's **Seasons** tab, press **Copy to next season**. It copies the dates a year on, with the same aircraft, crew numbers and rotation, and opens the new season.

### Confirm or cancel several seasons

Tick seasons on the mission's **Seasons** tab to confirm, cancel or delete them together.
