# Contact and feedback

Questions, problems and ideas about Zom's MRP all go to the same place, and every message is read.

## Send feedback from Zom's MRP

**Settings > About > Send feedback** starts an email to ZomBeatz Media. Write what happened or what you'd like, and send it.

Or email **[info@zombeatzmedia.com](mailto:info@zombeatzmedia.com)** directly.

## Reporting a bug

A good report gets a fix faster. Include:

1. **What you did**, step by step ("Opened a season, went to Pilots, pressed Fill the schedule").
2. **What happened**, and what you expected instead. Copy any message word for word.
3. **Your version** (**Settings > About**) and where it runs: Windows, Mac, Linux, a server, or a phone.
4. **A screenshot**, if it shows the problem. The [live demo](https://zombeatz.github.io/Zoms_MRP/) is a good place to show it without your own crew's names.

Never send passwords. Nobody at ZomBeatz Media will ever ask for them.

## Security problems

Please email rather than opening a public issue. See [Backups and security](./server-backups#reporting-a-security-problem).

## On GitHub

Zom's MRP is open source. On [GitHub](https://github.com/ZomBeatz/Zoms_MRP) you can report a problem or suggest a feature under **Issues**, see what's being worked on, and read the code.
