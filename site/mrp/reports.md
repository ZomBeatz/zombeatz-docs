# Reports and sharing

**Reports** shows fleet use, days per mission, how busy each person is and how much of the flying was fully crewed, for any dates, and turns it into pages to share.

![The Reports page](/img/mrp/reports.png)

## Picking the period

Pick one of the ready-made periods, **This week**, **Next week** and longer ones, or **Pick dates** for your own first and last day.

## What's on it

| Part | Shows |
|---|---|
| The tiles | Mission days, fleet on missions, fully crewed, crew busy |
| **Days by mission** | Each mission's days in the period |
| **Aircraft use** | Each tail's days on missions and in maintenance |
| **Missions in this period** | Each mission, how well it's crewed, and its cost, charge and margin when [rates](./costs) are set |
| Crew | How busy each person is |

## Pages to share

| Button | Makes |
|---|---|
| **PDF report** | A summary page, the fleet and crew boards colored like the app, the missions, workload, a training grid, maintenance and problems |
| **Excel report** | The same parts as sheets, each printing landscape, one page wide |
| **Week sheet** | One page for this week or next: each job's crew day by day, » where someone takes over, open seats in red, time off and maintenance |

They're saved to your Downloads folder.

## The whole plan in Excel

**Settings > Your data > Export to Excel** makes one workbook of the whole plan: the fleet board, fleet use, seasons, the crew roster, training, maintenance and problems.

## An Excel copy for Teams (desktop app)

The desktop app can keep an up-to-date Excel copy of the plan in a folder.

1. In Teams, open the channel's **Files** and choose **Sync**, so OneDrive keeps that folder on this computer.
2. In **Settings > Excel copy for Teams**, press **Choose a folder…** and pick it.

It's rewritten a few seconds after each change, so the team always has the latest. **Write it now**, **Show it**, **Choose another folder…** and **Stop keeping a copy** are beside it. The demo is never copied.

## A person's own schedule

**Their schedule (PDF)** on a person makes their next 90 days on a page. See [Crew and time off](./crew#their-schedule-as-a-pdf).
