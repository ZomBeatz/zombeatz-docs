# Mod installers (FOMOD)

Some mods come with an installer (a `fomod` folder) that asks which parts you want: a texture size, which patches, an optional feature.

## Going through one

In the [library](./library), a file with an installer has **Choose options**. Its steps open one after another, with the pictures and descriptions the author made; pick what you want on each. Choices that depend on other mods (a patch for a mod you have) are worked out for you, the way the installer means them to be.

What you picked is built into a folder of its own next to the mod, so the mod's own files never change. **Options…** on the file opens the installer again to change them. A profile with mods whose options haven't been chosen yet says so before Play.

## Your choices are kept

A new version of the mod remembers what you picked last time, so updating doesn't mean answering everything again.

## Collections

A [collection](./collections) brings its curator's choices, and they're used by themselves: you're not asked.

## Mods without an installer

Most mods have none and go straight in. A mod whose files sit a folder too deep is caught by the [conflict check](./conflict-check).
