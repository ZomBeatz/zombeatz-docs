# Mod packs and share codes

Share a profile without sharing mod files (sharing mods' files isn't allowed on Nexus Mods): a pack lists the mods, and the person opening it downloads them from Nexus Mods themselves.

## Mod packs

- **Save as mod pack…** on a profile saves a small `.json` file with the profile's mods, their files, their order and rules.
- **Open mod pack…** on the Profiles page makes a profile from one and queues every mod that isn't in the library yet.

Your own mod files (not from Nexus Mods) and Thunderstore mods are left out of Nexus packs.

## Share codes

A **share code** is the same thing as text you can paste in a chat: **Copy share code** on a profile, **Paste share code…** on the Profiles page. Codes start with `ZM1-`.

## Collections' add-ons

The add-ons on a [collection](./collections) profile can be shared on their own as a mod pack, so a friend with the same collection gets just your changes.
