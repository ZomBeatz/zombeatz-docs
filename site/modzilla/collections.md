# Collections

A **collection** on Nexus Mods is a curated list of mods for a game, with the curator's installer choices and load order. Zom's Modzilla downloads every file in it and makes a profile from it.

## Adding a collection

- On the collection's page on nexusmods.com, press its **Mod manager download** button (once the website's buttons open the app).
- Or paste its address in **Add mod…** (`https://www.nexusmods.com/games/<game>/collections/<id>`).

Every file is queued. **Premium** accounts download them all without clicks. **Free** accounts click **Slow download** for each one: the app opens the pages one after another, so it's a click per file and nothing else.

Files you already have aren't downloaded again, even if they came from another collection or by hand.

## The profile it makes

The collection becomes a profile with its mods in the curator's order, and the curator's installer choices are used by themselves: the installers don't ask you anything. Optional mods are left out unless you tick **Include the optional mods** when adding it.

Each collection profile shows its **revision** (its version on Nexus Mods). Files hosted outside Nexus Mods (on another site) have to be fetched by hand; the profile lists them with a link.

## Your changes

The collection as the curator made it is kept apart from your changes:

- Switch mods on or off, add your own, change the order: these are your changes, and the curator's list stays as it was underneath.
- **Add-ons** are named sets of your changes ("Better textures", "Extra quests"), each switched on or off, and shareable on their own as a mod pack.

## Newer revisions

When the curator publishes a newer revision, the profile says so. **Update this profile** queues what's new and changed and moves the profile to it. Your changes and add-ons carry over on top.
