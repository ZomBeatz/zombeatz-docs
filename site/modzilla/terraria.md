# Terraria and tModLoader

Terraria's mods aren't put in by copying files: they come from the Steam Workshop through **tModLoader** (a free app on Steam). Zom's Modzilla manages them through tModLoader's own list.

On the **Games** page, the **tModLoader (Terraria)** panel shows:

- every mod you have, switched on or off (written to tModLoader's `enabled.json`, keeping a backup of it first),
- **Workshop on Steam**, to subscribe to more mods,
- **Mod packs**: save the mods switched on now as a pack, and switch to a pack later.

Close tModLoader before switching mods.
