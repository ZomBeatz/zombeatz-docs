# Mod Organizer 2

Moving from Mod Organizer 2 (on Windows, or under Wine)? **From MO2…** on the Profiles page makes a Zom's Modzilla profile from an MO2 profile.

1. Pick MO2's folder (the one with `ModOrganizer.ini`, or a portable instance).
2. Pick the profile.
3. Zom's Modzilla reads its mod list in order, which mods are switched on, and each mod's Nexus Mods number and file.

Mods come from MO2's `mods` folder into your library, linked when they're on the same drive (no extra space) and copied when not. Nothing is downloaded again or asked of Nexus Mods: the numbers come from MO2's own notes, and MO2's folders are only read. Mods MO2 installed with an installer keep the files it chose. Mods without a Nexus Mods number become mods of your own.
