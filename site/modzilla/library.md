# The library

The **Library** page lists every mod you have, for the game picked in the sidebar (or every game).

![The Library](/img/modzilla/library.png)

## Where mods are kept

Each mod gets its own folder under its game, in the library folder (**Settings › Storage › Library folder**, `~/Games/Nexus Mods` at first):

```
~/Games/Nexus Mods/skyrimspecialedition/266 - SkyUI/
```

The folder holds the downloaded archive, the unpacked files, and a `nexus.json` saying what the mod is. A mod's files are never changed by putting it in a game: games get links to them.

**A folder of its own for a game** keeps one game's mods somewhere else, like the drive the game is on (an SD card, say). Moving copies everything first and only then deletes the old copy. When a game and its mods are on the same drive, mods are linked into the game and take no extra space; on different drives they're copied.

## Unpacking

With **Unpack downloads** on (the usual), zip, 7z and rar files are unpacked when they finish. **Keep archives after unpacking** keeps the original too, so you can unpack it again. Anything still packed is unpacked when a profile needs it, or with **Unpack** on the mod.

Mods with an installer (FOMOD) ask their questions when they go into a profile: see [Mod installers](./installers).

## Finding mods

Search by name, filter by tag, or show only what needs attention:

- **Not in any profile**: mods you have but don't use.
- **Gone from Nexus Mods**: mods their authors hid or removed. You keep your copy.
- Mods marked adult by Nexus Mods are tagged **Adult**; **Settings › Adult mods** can blur their pictures or hide them from Browse.

Sort by name, newest or biggest, and see how much space each takes.

## Updates

**Check for updates** looks for newer versions of the files you have, with **Get the update…** for each one, or all of them. Each mod's changelog shows what changed.

## Looking inside a mod

**Look inside** shows a mod's files and where each goes in the game, which ones lose to a later mod in a profile, and its pictures and readmes.

![Looking inside a mod](/img/modzilla/look-inside.png)

## Your own mod files

**Choose file…** on the Library page adds a mod from a zip, 7z or rar on your computer: one from another site, or your own. It works like any other mod, except it's never looked up on Nexus Mods or put in mod packs (others couldn't download it).

Files a modding tool makes can also become a mod of your own: see [Game tools](./game-tools#modding-tools).

## Tags and notes

Right-click a mod (or press **X** on a controller) for **Tags…** and **Add a note…**. Tags are yours to use however you like ("textures", "testing", "keep"); notes are for what to remember ("needs the 1.2 patch").

## Endorsing and tracking

When you're signed in, the 👍 button endorses a mod on Nexus Mods: it tells the author thanks. **Track on Nexus Mods** (right-click) adds it to your tracked mods on the website. Both are sent only when you press them.

## Deleting

**Delete mod…** removes a mod's folder from the library. Deleting one file of a mod keeps the others.
