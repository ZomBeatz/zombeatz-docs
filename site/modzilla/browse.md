# Browse

**Browse** shows mods for the game picked in the sidebar, with pictures.

## Nexus Mods

Three lists: **Trending**, **New**, and **Just updated**. Each mod shows its picture, author, summary and endorsements, and whether you have it already. **Get** downloads its main file (Premium), or opens the file's page for its **Slow download** button (free accounts). **Open page** opens the mod on nexusmods.com.

**Request saver** makes each list load only when you ask for it: each load is one request to Nexus Mods (the website itself costs nothing).

## Thunderstore

For games on Thunderstore (Valheim, Lethal Company, R.E.P.O. and 340+ more), Browse switches to Thunderstore's mods: search them, sort by **Popular**, **Top rated** or **Just updated**, and **Get** a mod with everything it needs. **Put into** sends it straight to a profile. See [Thunderstore and r2modman](./thunderstore).

## Adult mods

Mods Nexus Mods marks as adult follow **Settings › Adult mods**: shown, blurred, or left out of Browse.
